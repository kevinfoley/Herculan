# Known Issues

## Earthsiege 2 (original retail game)

_Some bugs listed in this section were tested on Windows 11. It's possible that some bugs would not occur on original 1990s hardware, though most bugs are verified in disassembly. Bugs in this section are reproduced as-is in HERCULAN Engine unless otherwise noted._

_Some of the bugs in this section were filed by Claude and are un-verified. Claude frequently concludes that a memory address/feature is unreachable and unused in retail, when in fact the mechanism for accessing this address/feature simply has not been found yet; any claim to this effect should be viewed with skepticism. Likewise, Claude sometimes confuses save files with the full set of retail missions, so any claim that a feature is never used in any retail mission should be viewed with skepticism._

_Note to Claude: Detailed technical descriptions belong in their respective docs, not here. Give a short plain-English summary._

### Cosmetic
- Samson cockpit: several HUD elements are slightly misaligned (shield balance text is not centered horizontally with meter; firing chain, LINK, and TRACK buttons are slightly too high and too far outward)
- The Herc Construction ("Build") screen shows two wrong chassis stats: the Outlaw's top speed as 80 kph where the manual and the in-game readout give 100, and the Raptor II's hardpoints as 4 where the machine has 5. **Fixed** in HERCULAN Engine as an optional Tweak. See [`docs/retail/formats/herc-catalogs.md`](docs/retail/formats/herc-catalogs.md#gamherc_infdat--the-chassis-stat-table).
- The cockpit computer never says `DAMAGE LEVEL CRITICAL` due to a typo in the code. **Fixed** in HERCULAN as an optional Tweak. See [`docs/retail/formats/cockpit-messages.md`](docs/retail/formats/cockpit-messages.md#posters).
- The compass's power-up wind-up only works in one direction, with rotation finishing instantly in the other direction. Reproduced as-is. Note that by design the windup animation is not played when piloting the Razor. See [`docs/retail/formats/cockpit-gunsight-hud.md`](docs/retail/formats/cockpit-gunsight-hud.md#power-up-wind-up).
- The HUD speed readout does not reflect speed accurately. Hercs have two strides, "walking" and "running". In the walking stride, the Herc moves much more slowly than the speed gauge indicates. Reproduced as-is; showing the real speed is an optional Tweak in HERCULAN Engine. See [`docs/retail/simulation/mech-locomotion.md`](docs/retail/simulation/mech-locomotion.md#walkrun-gait-discontinuity).
- The shield balance readout does not move in even steps of 20: one press forward from the centre reads 119/81 instead of 120/80, and so on up to 199/1. A press moves the balance slightly less than 20 points and the readout rounds the front number down. Verified in retail. Reproduced as-is; reading even steps is an optional Tweak in HERCULAN Engine. See [`docs/retail/formats/cockpit-hud-widgets.md`](docs/retail/formats/cockpit-hud-widgets.md#readouts--shieldsgauge_updatereadouts-00444a68).
- An ELF or ELF2 shot paints a stray orange-white dash at the muzzle, roughly four pixels wide, where the jagged tracer branch falls through into the straight-beam draw. See [`docs/retail/simulation/beam-visuals.md`](docs/retail/simulation/beam-visuals.md#the-muzzle-stub-is-a-retail-fall-through).
- In the low-memory mode (`-l`, or under 12 MB physical), catalog id `0x22`'s explosion sound fails to open and plays silently — its sample exists only in the `hmi\` bank, not the `hmx\` bank low-memory mode uses. Fixed in v1.10, which adds the missing sample. See [`docs/retail/formats/audio.md`](docs/retail/formats/audio.md#sample-banks).
- Turning MUSIC off in the preferences does not keep it off. Starting a mission forces the music flag back on after it has already decided not to play a track, so the CD stays quiet until anything resumes the sound — alt-tabbing away and back is enough — and then plays. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine. See [`docs/retail/formats/audio.md`](docs/retail/formats/audio.md#the-mission-session-overrides-the-music-preference).
- Setting PILOT MESSAGE to TEXT ONLY also silences the cockpit computer's voice, whatever COMPUTER MESSAGE says: the PILOT MESSAGE row switches off all recorded speech, not just the squad's. With COMPUTER MESSAGE on VOICE ONLY the computer then says nothing at all, in text or voice. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/formats/cockpit-messages.md`](docs/retail/formats/cockpit-messages.md#the-port).
- `Sound_Place`'s pan calculation snaps a sound placed exactly abeam on the listener's right to the wrong side: bearing zero truncates to the hard-*left* value where every neighbouring bearing on both sides yields hard-*right*. Not reproduced in HERCULAN Engine. See [`docs/retail/formats/audio.md`](docs/retail/formats/audio.md#playing-a-sound).
- A drop pod's whistle and landing play only in the left speaker, wherever the pod is. Both are played at the player's own camera rather than at the pod, and a sound with no direction is panned hard left. Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, which centres them; hearing them from the pod itself, louder and from kilometres off, is an optional Tweak. See [`docs/retail/formats/audio.md`](docs/retail/formats/audio.md#a-sound-played-at-the-camera).
- Behind the Preferences screen (F12) the camera circles the player's Herc by a fixed step every time the screen is redrawn, and nothing limits how often that is, so on a modern PC it spins far too fast. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, which turns it 25 steps a second, the rate the game itself runs at. See [`docs/retail/simulation/preferences.md`](docs/retail/simulation/preferences.md#preferences-and-controls-dbsimexe).
- The [F11] objectives panel prints its objective text five pixels right of centre. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/simulation/alert-panels.md`](docs/retail/simulation/alert-panels.md#the-objectives-panels-geometry).
- The Range readout of the MFD TARGET tab (F5) prints raw world units — 1 unit = 6 mm. Every other distance readout in the cockpit converts to metres first (`Hud_WorldUnitsToMetres`). Implemented as a **configurable tweak** in HERCULAN Engine.
- Samson: When firing lasers from the two lowest hardpoints, the beams emerge from a little above the barrels. Not sure if this happens with all lasers or if it's hardpoint specific. Only visible in third-person view.
- A Razor nacelle strike draws its impact effect at the **left** nacelle whichever nacelle was struck. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/simulation/razor-flight.md`](docs/retail/simulation/razor-flight.md#contact-probes).
- The speed bar of the cockpit's throttle gauge never moves in a Razor: the gauge is fed the walker's speed value, which flight never updates. The throttle bar itself works. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/simulation/razor-flight.md`](docs/retail/simulation/razor-flight.md#flight-state--mech0x2b9).
- The MFD TARGET screen (F5) can never read `SHIELDS DN` for a HERC target, because that status is latched only on the player's own Herc for the STATUS (F1) screen. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/formats/mfd.md`](docs/retail/formats/mfd.md#viewport-and-condition-per-class).
- The cockpit's power-up announces `INTERNAL DAMAGE DETECTED` for any damage at all to the first ten internals, however slight: its threshold of 90 is tested against a condition index that only runs from 0 to 4, so it always passes. Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/formats/cockpit-messages.md`](docs/retail/formats/cockpit-messages.md#posters).
- The repair screen prints nonsense for a badly damaged component. Its Condition readout has six damage levels but only four words to name them, so a component between 1% and 29% reads `% Complete` and a destroyed one reads `Unassigned` — the next two unrelated entries in the text table. Verified in retail. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#the-condition-readout).
- A second hit on the cockpit stops the damage shake instead of restarting it. Taking a hit shakes the view and flashes the palette for about a second; a second hit inside that window clears the shake and extends the timer, but the check that would set it going again only passes when no shake is running — so the view goes still for the rest of the window. The flash carries on if the second hit lands while the normal palette is showing, and stops on the normal palette if it lands while the flash colours are. Both halves are reproduced as-is; a flash that always carries on is an optional Tweak in HERCULAN Engine. See "The damage shake" in [`docs/retail/formats/cockpit-canopy-palette.md`](docs/retail/formats/cockpit-canopy-palette.md#the-damage-shake).
- The repair screen's damage diagram draws the torsos and the legs on opposite sides: Left Torso is on the viewer's left while Left Leg is on the viewer's right, and vice-versa. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#the-damage-diagram).
- A shell tab picked with the **right** mouse button opens its screen but is left drawn unlit, and right-clicking the tab already open unlights it. The button fires on the release and then redraws the tab as released, after the tab has already marked itself as the open one. Verified in retail. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#the-widget-that-takes-a-click-decides-what-it-does).
- The mission screen's summary text miscounts its pages: a text that exactly fills its pages gets an extra empty page the down arrow scrolls to, and a text one line short of a full page cannot be paged at all (harmless, since it fits on the one page). Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#the-summary-text-box).
- After scrapping a weapon in the armory, its row is drawn as though it were no longer selected, all but its count, yet it still is: with weapons built by hand the next click on it queues a unit instead of selecting it. Verified in retail. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#the-scrap-dialog).
- The HDD damage detail (F8) prints `NO TARGET SELECTED` / `NO INFO AVAILABLE` 40 pixels right of the screen's centre (in 640x480), on a yellow box shifted the same way. Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/formats/heads-down-display.md`](docs/retail/formats/heads-down-display.md#subject).
- On the HDD damage detail's weapons view (F8, `W`), an empty hardpoint's row can show another weapon's name once the arrows have stepped through other HERCs: the row reuses a name slot left over from an earlier HERC. Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine — the row stays blank. See [`docs/retail/formats/heads-down-display.md`](docs/retail/formats/heads-down-display.md#damage-detail--page-1).
- VSHELL's Preferences screen offers three repair modes, but the repair screen only has a line to print for the first two, so choosing "Manually Repair All Hercs" leaves the readout showing whatever it last held — `Manual Repair` unless the mode was Auto earlier in the session. Reproduced, except that HERCULAN Engine goes back to `Manual Repair` after a game is loaded. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#repairing-and-cancelling).
- The REPAIR screen names a Herc's parts after the last Herc picked on the REPAIR tab itself: entering the tab does not update them, so a Razor picked on WEAPONS, BUILD or CREW is listed with torsos and legs (and a walker picked after a Razor with nacelles and wings) until another Herc is clicked in the REPAIR roster. Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, which names every Herc by its own chassis. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#which-names-a-chassis-shows).
- On the REPAIR screen, when a Tomahawk is selected, some weapons are drawn with the wrong position (and possibly the wrong graphic) when attached to the head hardpoint, such as the ELF, ELF2, and EMP2. Verified in retail. Reproduced as-is.
- A campaign debrief in chapters 1 to 3 mentions at most one kind of loss. Hercs lost, squadmates lost and squadmates the player killed all write the same line, so only the last that applies shows. When the player killed exactly two squadmates, "You killed TWO squadmates!" is replaced by its own follow-on, " Good luck in the Brig, soldier.", which is all the line says; killing more than two goes unmentioned, leaving the line to report only Hercs or squadmates lost, if any. Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/simulation/impact-effects.md`](docs/retail/simulation/impact-effects.md#tick--explosion_tickupdate-0040813c).

### Functional
- Player Herc acceleration/deceleration and turning are framerate-dependent (**fixed** in HERCULAN Engine)
- The "center legs" function moves the turret awkwardly and does not center the legs perfectly.
- Scrapping a Herc that is still under construction pays either nothing or its full price, never anything between: the yield is computed as `((100 - percentComplete) / 100) * price`, and that integer division collapses to zero for every progress figure from 1 to 99. A chassis stands at 0% only between its purchase and the next debrief, so the full price comes back only for undoing a purchase. Confirmed in the disassembly. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/shell/armory.md`](docs/retail/shell/armory.md#scrapping).
- A squad pilot's skill rises for scoring nothing. Skill steps up when career Herc + Flyer kills hit a multiple of the pilot's divisor, and zero is a multiple — so a pilot who has never killed a machine is promoted after every mission survived until they reach ELITE. Confirmed in the disassembly: only the division remainder is tested, and nothing checks whether the kill total is zero. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/shell/campaign-loop.md`](docs/retail/shell/campaign-loop.md#pilot-progression).
- Some buildings' collision volumes are shorter than the visible mesh, so shots pass through the top of the building.
- Turning with the keyboard turns at half of the speed of turning with the joystick.
- The turret motion stutters when aiming with a joystick. The drawn turret pose is quantized to about 0.45°, noticeable only with small analog inputs. **Fixed** in HERCULAN Engine by default; the residual step is about five times finer. See [`docs/retail/simulation/torso-aim.md`](docs/retail/simulation/torso-aim.md#angle-to-pose).
- A gun mount that has taken damage fires **faster**, not slower: past half damage, each further step shortens its refire delay, so a badly damaged autocannon refires up to twice as fast. Verified with disassembly. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/simulation/weapon-mounts.md`](docs/retail/simulation/weapon-mounts.md#losing-a-mount).
- ATC75, ATC100 and L500 hit exactly as hard as ATC35, ATC50 and L300, and L400 only slightly harder than L200. A fired shot looks its projectile record up again by class and subtype id and takes the first match, so the four weapons' own damage figures in `PROJ.DAT` are never applied; L400 keeps only its own higher power. The four also fire those weapons' rounds and beams, though `BULLETS.DAT` and `BEAM.DAT` hold their own. Verified with disassembly and measured in retail. **Fixed** in HERCULAN Engine as a Tweak that is on by default. See [`docs/retail/formats/proj-dat.md`](docs/retail/formats/proj-dat.md#lookup).
- Laying a ground shape while the 150-slot pool is full should crash the game — a drop pod landing, a HERC spawning (a shadow per foot and one for the body) or an impact effect whose type asks for one: none of the three checks whether it got a shape before writing to it. Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine — the spawn goes without the shape. See [`docs/retail/simulation/ground-shapes.md`](docs/retail/simulation/ground-shapes.md#the-class).
- The impact point of a machine-versus-machine collision mixes two coordinate frames. On level ground near sea level the two nearly agree; on a hill the blast goes off well below the machines, and its falloff reaches their legs rather than their torsos. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/simulation/damage-system.md`](docs/retail/simulation/damage-system.md#a-collision--mech_collisiontest-00418f74).
- Starting a sound retunes any copy of it that is already playing. Volume and stereo position belong to the catalog entry rather than to the individual playback, and get applied to whichever copy is sounding at the moment the next one starts — so a nearby footstep jumps to the volume and position of a distant one that follows it. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/formats/audio.md`](docs/retail/formats/audio.md#a-repeated-play-layers-it-does-not-restart).
- The one looping sound shared by every fire in the mission is placed on whichever fire is nearest the camera, but the distance behind that choice is computed in 32-bit arithmetic that overflows past about 278 m, so a building burning beside the player can fall silent because something is burning a quarter-mile off. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine. See [`docs/retail/simulation/destruction-effects.md`](docs/retail/simulation/destruction-effects.md#where-the-shared-sound-is-heard).
- VIEW DEMO plays its recording as fast as the computer can draw it, and nothing paces it to real time, so on a modern PC a demo two to six minutes long is over in moments. Verified in retail. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, which plays a tape at the speed it was recorded. See [`docs/retail/formats/tap-input-tape.md`](docs/retail/formats/tap-input-tape.md#timing).
- In VSHELL, the "Saved Games" and "Preferences" panels aren't centered vertically.
- In a practice mission, the last wingman the mission gives you stays behind: the step that puts each wingman on duty runs one wingman late, so the last never goes, and a mission with one wingman is flown alone. Confirmed against a retail practice handoff. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#starting-a-practice-mission).
- Opening the WEAPONS or REPAIR tab without a usable Herc selected picks the first finished Herc using the rules of the tab you just left instead of its own: coming from CREW with a squad member's row selected, a Razor is refused and the tab opens with no Herc. Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, which applies each tab's own rule. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#entering-the-weapons-screen).
- On the WEAPONS screen, with a hardpoint selected, clicking a weapon you have none of unequips the hardpoint instead of being refused whenever the weapon's number matches the hardpoint's guidance setting: a 20, 35 or 50 mm autocannon on a missile rack (or the Razor's launcher) set to ARH, ARM or EO, and the 100 mm autocannon on a hardpoint holding any other weapon. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#fitting-a-weapon).
- Taking a damaged weapon off a Herc repairs it for free: the weapon goes back into the inventory at the condition it had when it was fitted, normally 100%, and the hardpoint's damage is thrown away. With the hardpoint selected on the WEAPONS screen, clicking the weapon it already carries does the same in one click. A salvaged weapon works the other way round, losing any repairs made to it on the Herc and going back to the condition it was salvaged at. Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#fitting-a-weapon).
- On the WEAPONS screen, after changing Herc or re-entering the tab, weapons the previously selected hardpoint could not take can stay greyed out until another row is clicked. Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md`](docs/retail/shell/screen-layout.md#fitting-a-weapon).
- The memory manager's check for a write past the end of a block never actually looks past the block, so such an overrun goes unreported until it corrupts something else. Read from the disassembly. No bearing on HERCULAN Engine, which has its own memory management. See [`docs/retail/runtime-library.md`](docs/retail/runtime-library.md#the-overrun-check-checks-nothing).
- File errors all get the same generic failure code, and the "bad file handle" check can never trigger: the code tests the address of the error variable's import stub instead of the error value. Read from the disassembly. No bearing on HERCULAN Engine, which has its own file handling. See [`docs/retail/runtime-library.md`](docs/retail/runtime-library.md#the-errno-test-reads-an-import-stub).
- In v1.10, the release on the GoldGames disc ([`docs/retail/retail-builds.md`](docs/retail/retail-builds.md)), the on-line manual's `Readme` button, and the three Readme links in its text, open `esreadme.txt`, which that release's installer never creates: its `BATCH.EXE` copies the chosen language's `README.WRI` to `readme.txt`, where v1.0's copies it to `esreadme.txt`. - In v1.10, opening the campaign map can close the game: the shell builds the name of the map's text file on top of whatever was left in that memory, and when it was not empty the file is not found and the shell exits. Read from the disassembly, not checked in retail. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine. See [`docs/retail/retail-builds.md`](docs/retail/retail-builds.md#v110s-shell-reads-the-language-twice-more).
- A Razor tests the ground for its left wing and its cockpit at the wrong points — the left wing well behind the aircraft on its centreline, the cockpit off to the right of the nose — and every airframe contact check but the fuselage's looks where the aircraft was before that tick's move. See [`docs/retail/simulation/razor-flight.md`](docs/retail/simulation/razor-flight.md#the-probes-share-one-transform).
- Buildings that a mission holds back until the player comes near are there from the start, invisible but solid: a HERC walks into walls it cannot see. In `C5_04` a player approaching one of the Cybrid bases from the north can be stopped by its northernmost buildings, unseen, just before reaching the area that reveals them. - Mission music plays once and then stops instead of looping. The game waits for Windows to say the CD track has finished, and on Windows 11 the CD driver never says so. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, which plays the disc's audio itself. See [`docs/retail/formats/audio.md`](docs/retail/formats/audio.md#cd-audio).

### AI
- When an AI machine picks which part of its target to shoot at, it looks at the damage on **its own** parts instead of the target's — so it works at whichever of its own components is worst hurt, limited to parts the target still has. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/simulation/ai-targeting.md`](docs/retail/simulation/ai-targeting.md#which-component-the-shot-is-aimed-at--mech_aiselectaimcomponent-0041ce08).
- Two AI machines compare their combat ratings through a random jitter that was meant to be `rand % 1000` but is written `rand & 1000`, so the jitter lands on only 64 of the 1001 numbers in that range. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/simulation/ai-targeting.md`](docs/retail/simulation/ai-targeting.md#relative-combat-rating).
- A mission group only ever walks the route attached to its **first** order. Later orders carry routes of their own and several shipped missions set them, but the group's route is loaded once when it is built and nothing found re-points it or resets the group's place on it, so a group that moves on to a second movement order carries on along the first order's route, with no waypoints left at all if the first order ran it out. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/simulation/ai-goals.md`](docs/retail/simulation/ai-goals.md#the-route-cursor-is-loaded-once).
- A live group of HERCs with no orders at all would crash the game on its first AI frame: the state-selection function falls through with a null behaviour descriptor and dereferences it. None of the 62 retail `.MSN` missions has one: every group of HERCs or flyers has an order in its first slot, and only structure groups go without (structures have no AI). Two of the code paths into the crash are traced, and what the other seven would hand over is an open item in the doc. HERCULAN Engine leaves such a machine in the state it already held. See [`docs/retail/simulation/ai-goals.md`](docs/retail/simulation/ai-goals.md#a-group-with-no-order-at-all).
- For **flyer** groups, the `ram`, `guard` and `follow` orders are unimplemented, and so is an empty order slot: the state-selection function has no case for any of them and hands the behaviour installer a stale register that holds its own code address, so the aircraft is put in a "state" made of opcode bytes and calls a wild address on the same tick. None of the 76 flyer groups in the 62 retail `.MSN` missions carries one: each has an order in its first slot, and the verbs used are `search/destroy`, `patrol` and `sleep`. HERCULAN Engine ignores these orders and leaves the aircraft in the state it had. See [`docs/retail/simulation/ai-flyers.md`](docs/retail/simulation/ai-flyers.md#orders--flyer_aiselectbehaviour-00422d00).
- A flyer group put to sleep never wakes: a sleeping aircraft never re-reads its group's orders, so moving the group on to a next order leaves it asleep. No retail mission reaches it: the one flyer group that sleeps (`C1_03.MSN`) has a single order, and the seven flyer groups with two orders both `search/destroy`. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/simulation/ai-flyers.md`](docs/retail/simulation/ai-flyers.md#the-seven-states).
- A group under a `travelling` order can skip waypoints. Every other movement state has the group leader alone walk the route while the rest hold formation, but `travelling` has every member walk it, and all of them step the one shared cursor — so a tightly-packed group crossing closely-spaced waypoints advances past several of them in a tick. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/retail/simulation/ai-navigation.md`](docs/retail/simulation/ai-navigation.md#travelling-9-and-bulldog-travel-11--mech_behaviourtravelthink-0041d9cc).

## HERCULAN Engine

_Note to Claude: This section is for listing features which have been implemented but behave differently than retail or otherwise incorrectly. Features that haven't been tackled yet go in [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md). Bugs in retail belong in the previous section. Detailed technical descriptions belong in documentation, not here. Give a short plain-English summary._

- Terrain detail-texture scatter may still differ from retail: which cells draw the theater bank's alternate frame is a random roll whose alignment with DBSIM's own sequence isn't confirmed. See "Detail-texture scatter RNG" in [`docs/retail/formats/terrain-texturing.md`](docs/retail/formats/terrain-texturing.md#retail-numbers).
- (Deferred) Distance fog on terrain is smooth where retail's steps cell by cell. The amount of fog and the ground it lands on match; the flat step across each cell is missing. See "Engine port" in [`docs/retail/formats/distance-fog-and-sky.md`](docs/retail/formats/distance-fog-and-sky.md).
- (Accepted) Past about 51 degrees of roll, retail's horizon line ends inside the view and its sky stops short of the view's sides; the engine's sky always reaches them. No HERC rolls that far. See [`docs/retail/formats/distance-fog-and-sky.md`](docs/retail/formats/distance-fog-and-sky.md#the-horizon-line).
- `TSGouraudPoly` faces fade by an RGB blend toward the fog colour rather than by a ramp depth slice. Every other surface fogs through the ramp as retail does. Same section as above.
- Gouraud-shaded structures band differently from retail on some geometry, e.g. the type-15 octagonal tower: retail's wide dark bands are not reproducible under the engine's current shading mechanism, and the cause is unresolved. See "Type-15 band widths" in [`docs/retail/formats/dts-texture-binding.md`](docs/retail/formats/dts-texture-binding.md#type-15-band-widths). Reference captures: `Reference/Gouraud_shading_comparison_2.png`, `Reference/Scramble_Training_Base_4.png`.
- (Accepted) Textures on shapes and on distant terrain cells are mapped perspective-correct, where retail spreads them evenly across the screen, so on a face that recedes steeply the texture sits slightly differently than in retail. Only nearby terrain cells are perspective-correct in retail. Deliberate: retail interpolates across each whole polygon row by row, which triangles on a GPU cannot reproduce without a seam along a quad's diagonal. See [`docs/retail/formats/dts-texture-binding.md`](docs/retail/formats/dts-texture-binding.md#screen-linear-and-perspective-correct-fills).
- The MFD's missile camera draws every machine and shape at the level of detail chosen for the cockpit view, where retail chooses it per view, so something near the round but far from the player is drawn coarser than retail draws it. See [`docs/retail/formats/mfd.md`](docs/retail/formats/mfd.md#mfdmissileview--mode-5).
- When projectiles hit buildings, many hit effects seem to clip inside the building - I don't observe this in retail. **Hit geometry ruled out**, and **building LOD ruled out** (the engine draws maximum detail, which is what retail's screenshots were taken at). Most likely a draw order issue.
- (Deferred) Due to nearest-neighbor scaling, text often looks bad when window height isn't an integer multiple of 240.
- (Needs re-verification) In the Scramble practice mission while piloting an Apocalypse, a Particle Beam Weapon is equipped to slot 8. In HERCULAN, when this PBW is fired the beam visibly clips off near the corner of the screen. This may be a camera near-clip plane issue.

Retail suppresses the computer's line, and its alert tone, while the external view is up (the line is still spoken), and paints neither message box while a view change is pending. See "The port" in [`docs/retail/formats/cockpit-messages.md`](docs/retail/formats/cockpit-messages.md#the-port).

A glance (`[F9]`/`[F10]`, the hat, the side screen edges) therefore slides the three panels only until the side panel's outer edge meets the window's edge; retail slides one whole panel. In a 4:3 window the two are the same, and in a wider one the glance is shorter and leaves part of the forward view on screen. The side screen-edge strips sit on the window's edges rather than on the forward view's, which here are mid-screen, and the two system buttons at the window's top-right corner.
