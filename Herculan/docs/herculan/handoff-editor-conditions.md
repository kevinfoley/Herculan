# Handoff — conditions, variants and Present when in the editor

Which version of a mission the editor shows, and how it edits row 1's flag tests and draws, variant tables, and which versions a record is present in. Part of the [editor handoff](handoff-editor.md). How conditions and draws resolve, and what moves the draws, is [Conditions and variants](handoff-editor-document.md#conditions-and-variants); which records each tool writes is [The tools](handoff-editor-document.md#the-tools).

## What the editor shows

The editor shows one version of a mission, the one its open generates, the same on every open. An open (File > Load from VOL, File > New mission) draws through `MissionOpener` from a generator whose every step is 0 (`SimRandom.Lowest`, the editor's choice): every row-1 draw at 0, so each range over a draw holding 0 holds; every variant table on the option a draw of 0 picks, the first in row-1 order for each of the 252 retail tables under a key; flags 4 to 6 at 0; and a training load's new career and squad build drawn from the same zeros. The flags are the open's: in training mode (practice, `INSTANT ACTION`, a new mission, one `gam\career.dat` does not list) the array is cleared and flags 3 to 6 seeded (`ShellTrainingLaunch.SeedDrawnFlags`); at a campaign position it starts empty, as at a career with no history, the load writing the position into flags 1 and 2 and seeding 3 to 6 likewise. The game's own launches draw from `ShellTrainingLaunch.StartupRandom`.

A herc, flyer, structure, ground vehicle, group, route or trigger area that version leaves out still stands in the scene, placed from the `.MSN` by its base, and every view, an objective's, a trigger's, a countdown's and an order slot's included, shows its record in full from the layout, opening with its [Present when](#present-when) ([Selection and panels](handoff-editor-viewing.md#selection-and-panels)). The outliner lists every group, trigger area, trigger, countdown, route and objective in its place whatever its condition, and marks the row of one not in every version "(not always present)", so one a Present when took out can be selected and brought back.

## Conditions

The Conditions list, under the collapsed **Advanced** header at the end of the Mission panel, shows every row-1 record in file order as "Condition <GUID>: <test>" (`ConditionEditing.Describe`: "flag 100 > 0", "draw below 11", "draw 0 to 9 of condition 66", "variant table 170 below 100", with "if condition 65" after one that requires another), each operator worded as the load tests flag against value, flag on the left. Nothing in the list creates a condition; Present when makes the flag tests and draws it needs ([What a set writes](#what-a-set-writes)), and a draw's view adds a range over it.

The view (`ConditionView`, through `ConditionEditor`) opens with "Condition <GUID>" and its kind, then:

- a flag test's flag, picked from the campaign variable list Present when uses ([The campaign variable picker](#the-campaign-variable-picker)), its operator and its value;
- a draw's bound; adding or deleting a draw, or changing whether one is made, changes every later draw and pick, and a draw's view says so;
- a range's draw (a choice of the draws before it, with a link) and its two ends, with a note when the ends lie past the draw's bound;
- Requires: none or any condition before it, with a link (a range's draw is its requirement, and it has no other). Row 1 is read in order, each record against the survivors before it, so a later one is refused with the reason and not offered;
- Used by: every record whose condition references the GUID, in rows 1 to 17 (row 5's 64-byte records by their first word) and the `.ENG`, one short line each, named from the layout as everywhere ([Names](handoff-editor-mission-logic.md#names)), a link where a view exists ("Condition 7", "Cybrid hercs (group 12)", "SCARAB in Cybrid hercs (group 5)", "2nd order of Cybrid hercs (group 4)", and the Mission view for the text package its lines edit) and plain text otherwise ("Text line 204", a herc no group places). A record of a row the editor has no view for is named by what it does, counted, with "(not shown in the editor)" ([`../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md`](../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md)): a header patch "Mission settings, such as the zone", since it sets the header's words and clears the campaign variables it lists as the mission loads; a row-3 value "A briefing or debrief movie"; another text package "Another version of the mission's lines and briefing movie"; a row-5 record "The debrief: its lines and movie"; a point "A position"; a heading "A heading". Options of random choices and orders no group runs are counted, and the variant alternatives it conditions counted apart, since no version places an alternative;
- Delete, refused while Used by lists anything.

No retail mission carries a row-1 GUID twice, and the view edits no condition whose GUID is. A variant table, and a range over one choosing an option the table reads, open the table's [Random choice view](#variants), the range's option highlighted; a range choosing no option the table reads is shown as any other range, read-only.

## Variants

A variant table is a **random choice**, and each range over it, with the record it conditions, one of its **options**. The Mission panel lists every one after Objectives under **Random choices**, in row-1 order, by its title and how many records pick from it ("Random herc: SCARAB or DIABLO (used by 3)"). Selecting one, its condition, or a range over it (from a link or the Conditions list) opens its Random choice view (`VariantView`, through `VariantEditor`, read from the `.MSN` and its layout by `RandomChoices`), the range's option highlighted:

- its title, built from its options: "Random herc: SCARAB or DIABLO", each type once, three at most and then "or N more"; "Random ground vehicle: ...", "Random route: 3 options";
- emphasized, a warning when it has no effect in play ([below](#when-a-random-choice-has-no-effect));
- each option on a line, numbered from 1: its chance, a percent to one decimal in a typed field; what it is, with what tells it from the others (a herc's chassis, and its weapons when another option has that chassis; a flyer's type; a structure's or ground vehicle's type and the name the MFD prints; a route's waypoint count and length, and its first waypoint when another option matches both); "(in the scene)" after the option the records picking from it show, the first ([What the editor shows](#what-the-editor-shows)); Remove;
- Nothing, the share left over, and what a load landing there does;
- Add option;
- **Used by**: every layout entry of rows 6 to 8 and 12 to 15 one of whose records picks by the table's key, whatever its condition, one identity link per line in game terms with the option the scene shows it with ("SCARAB 2 of 3 (group 4): shows option 1", "The player's HERC", "Squadmate 2's HERC", "Route 3", "SCARAB (no group places it)"), and the variant alternatives that pick from it themselves, counted;
- Delete random choice;
- **Advanced**, collapsed: its condition number and key, its requirement with a link, its roll, each option's condition number (a link, which highlights that option) and range, and any other table under the same key, as links.

A table the editor shows only has no control and says why in plain words.

**Chances.** An option's share is the draws its range holds that no range before it holds, out of the bound. A table opens with its shares rounded to one decimal adding up to the rounded total, largest fractions first, with a note giving them exactly where they are not (`VariantEditing.Percents`, `Exact`); `TRAIN5`'s table of eight options below 400 shows 12.5% each, exactly. A chance edit writes every chance as shown: the ranges contiguous from 0 in row-1 order, over the bound 100 when every chance is a whole percent and 1000 otherwise (`VariantEditing.ChanceBound`). A draw below any bound of 1 or more is one step of VSHELL's generator (`ShellRandom_Below`, [`../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md`](../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md#the-shells-generator)), so writing the bound moves no later pick, and no retail table draws below less than 1. The chances add up to 100% at most. A draw in what is left over takes no option, so a record picking it keeps its own fields, and the point, heading and actions those reference are not looked up, since a keyed record's refs are resolved only in what it copies (`MissionGenerator.ReadHercs` and its siblings), as the view says for a herc, flyer, structure or ground vehicle.

**Add option** copies the option the scene shows and gives it half the largest chance, rounded down to one decimal, taken from that option, the first of a tie (the editor's choice); it is refused when no chance is 0.2% or more. **Remove** gives the option's chance to the one before it, or after it for the first (the editor's choice), and is refused for the last. **Delete random choice** is refused while any record, in any row and whatever its condition, has the table's key (those under Used by), or anything else references the table, those listed as links.

### When a random choice has no effect

A squad HERC's pick is not used where the launch's rule gives the squad other machines or leaves the HERC out ([The player's squad](handoff-editor-roster.md#the-players-squad)): in a campaign mission, whose squad pilots the career's HERCs; the player's HERC on practice rows 4 to 7, where the player pilots a stock fit of the practice screen's Herc Type; the last squadmate a training load lists, who stays behind; and a squadmate after a gap in group 0's member slots, which no squad build reads. Used by says so under that record's line, and the view warns, emphasized, when every record picking from the choice is one of these ("No effect in play: the player pilots the Herc Type chosen on the practice screen.").

### Which tables are edited

A table is edited when each of its ranges conditions one GUID `-1` record of row 12, 13 or 14 that draws nothing itself, all of one row, no other table carries its key, and the squad draws nothing from it. Any other is shown with its reason and no control: route tables, tables under a shared key (`C1_05`'s conditions 36 and 40 share key 172, the second requiring a flag test and reached only by draws the first leaves uncovered), and the training missions' squad tables. Of the 783 retail ranges over a table, 694 choose a herc, 30 a flyer, 12 a structure and 47 a route (`HercWorks.Query conditions`).

### The Random choice line

The view of a herc, flyer, structure or ground vehicle outside the squad shows, after its Present when, **Random choice:** None, each edited table whose options are of its row, by its title, or New random choice from this herc (flyer, structure, ground vehicle), with a link to the choice and the option the scene shows it with ("The scene shows option 2: DIABLO."), or that it shows none of them and keeps its own details. A squad herc that picks from a choice shows the link and its option, with no choice. A choice is refused for a GUID two of whose records carry a key. New random choice's single option is a copy of what the scene shows the record as (the option its draw shows in the layout, else its own fields; `VariantEditing.DrawOf`), so it looks the same. None leaves the record as shown, keeping as its own the span it copied (the editor's choice). Each of the three changes whether the record draws, which shifts which option each later random choice picks on a given load but changes no odds and nothing the editor shows, so the view does not mention it (the editor's choice).

A route whose waypoints are an option says "Its waypoints are option N of" with a link to its choice, and that editing them changes that option wherever it is picked.

### Editing a record that picks

A record picking from a table takes its position and its engaged and defeated actions from the option, so a move or an action set writes an overlay of its own. Its type, and a herc's weapons and guidance, are the option's: where a field comes from an edited table's option (`VariantEditing.WhyNoDrawnField`), the view offers "Changes apply to:" "that option, wherever it is picked (N hercs)", N being the layout entries one of whose draws offers that option (`RandomChoices.Picking`), which writes the option itself, so each of them changes; or "only this herc" (flyer, structure, ground vehicle), back to the option whenever another record's view is shown and not saved, which writes the record's overlay instead, by the squad's rules for a value an overlay cannot carry ([The player's squad](handoff-editor-roster.md#the-players-squad)). `RosterFit` (Core, `Data/File/Msn/RosterFit.cs`) routes each field on its own, so a field the record carries itself is written there; a herc view whose fields are partly the option's and partly its own says "What was set for this herc alone stays as set." A Cybrid's hardpoints take the same choice ([A Cybrid's weapons](handoff-editor-roster.md#a-cybrids-weapons)).

Gaps:

- Only a type and a herc's weapons and guidance are written in the option; its other fields are not edited.
- A table's requirement is shown and not set; a new table requires nothing.
- A route keeps the waypoint list it picks, edited as the option every route picking it shares; route and squad tables are read-only.
- A route that picks from a choice the editor does not edit shows no Random choice line.

## Present when

The view of a herc, flyer, structure or ground vehicle, a group and an objective shows, after its name, which versions of the mission the record is present in, and each order slot after its other fields (`PresentWhenView`, through `PresenceEditor`). Everything is set in that view:

- a sentence, emphasized: "Present: always.", "Present only when campaign variable 30 is more than 0, and then in about 50% of those loads." (the roll is made only where the variables hold), "Present in about 50% of loads.";
- **Always**, **Only when a campaign variable...** or **In some loads...**, then a row per clause. A variable clause is a campaign variable ([the picker](#the-campaign-variable-picker)), a test (is, is not, is less than, is at most, is more than, is at least: the operators as the load tests flag against value, flag on the left) and a value, with **Remove**. The chance clause is "In about [N]% of loads", 1 to 99, with **Remove**. **And also when...** adds a variable row, written once a variable is picked, as "is more than 0" (`ConditionEditing.NewOperator` and `NewValue`); **And only in some loads...** adds the one chance, at 50% (the editor's choice). Choosing In some loads... keeps the chance and drops the variables. Adding or removing a chance adds or removes a draw, which shifts which option each later random choice draws on a given load but changes no odds and nothing the editor shows, so the view does not mention it (the editor's choice);
- when the record's details change between versions, a sentence per condition naming what changes, by the record types' field decodes (`RecordAspects`): "Its chassis and weapons change when campaign variable 250 is 1.", or "Some of its details change ..." where no field it sets has a name a designer knows; then that an edit in this view does not change those details in those versions ([Which record an edit writes](handoff-editor-document.md#which-record-an-edit-writes));
- **Advanced**, collapsed: the condition its records carry, a choice of Always and the mission's flag tests and ranges over a draw named as the Conditions list names them ("Condition 5: flag 30 > 0"), with a link; each record's own condition when they differ ("Record 2: condition 5 (flag 30 > 0)"); and, for a record that draws a variant, that every later pick changes where it is absent. A draw, a variant table and a range over one are not offered.

A change keeps the record selected. Where the mission as opened then leaves the record out, its view keeps showing its name and its Present when ([What the editor shows](#what-the-editor-shows)).

### The campaign variable picker

A dropdown (`CampaignVariablePicker`), which a flag test's view uses for its flag too, and an action's or objective's writes for theirs ([Campaign variable writes](handoff-editor-mission-logic.md#campaign-variable-writes)), with a box at its top that filters it as the user types:

- **Set by the game**, by what they hold (`CampaignVariableLabels`): 0 the outcome of the previous mission, whose clause is a choice of Won and Not won in place of a test and a value (written `> 0` and `< 1`, the tests of it retail writes most; any test holding for exactly one of 1 and 0 reads as that one), the results' outcome being whether the mission's last objective check answered success ([`../retail/simulation/mission-objectives.md`](../retail/simulation/mission-objectives.md#what-the-mission-leaves-the-shell--mission_writeresults-0042412c)); 1 the campaign chapter (a career stage: 1 to 5 for the chapters, 0 for practice) and 2 the mission index in it, the first 0 ([`../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md`](../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md#the-campaign-table--gamcareerdat)), which no retail mission tests; 4 to 6 three random numbers picked at each load, 0 to 11; 8 the squad HERCs the debrief wrote off for coming back below 30 mean condition ([`../retail/shell/armory.md`](../retail/shell/armory.md#scrapping)), not those the armory's Scrap button scraps, and 9 the squadmates killed, their condition at 0 ([`../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md`](../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md#pilot-progression)), both after the previous mission; 10 the squadmates the player took down (disabled or destroyed) in the previous mission ([`../retail/simulation/mission-deployment.md`](../retail/simulation/mission-deployment.md#the-mission-counters--dat_004a9ef4)). Flag 3 is not listed. Every other value is a number.
- **Read by the game after the mission**, by what the debrief does with each as the mission left it (`CampaignVariableLabels.Effect`), with what in this mission touches it: 20 the salvage bonus, 25 tons for each 1 ([`../retail/simulation/mission-deployment.md`](../retail/simulation/mission-deployment.md#the-mission-counters--dat_004a9ef4)); 21 to 42 the units of a weapon, by its armory name, that the debrief adds to the armory ("ATC50 units the debrief adds to the armory"); 50 to 53 and 56 to 59 a weapon the debrief unlocks when the variable holds a set value ([`../retail/formats/weapons-dat.md`](../retail/formats/weapons-dat.md#campaign-grants--armory_grantcampaignweapons-004126be)), and 60 to 63 a chassis likewise ([`../retail/formats/herc-catalogs.md`](../retail/formats/herc-catalogs.md#chassis-unlocks--herc_grantunlocks-004118c5)). Each label says what the game does with the variable, which holds for a test in a later mission too: there the variable carries what the missions before left, since the debrief does not clear 21 to 42 and the simulator zeroes 20 to 42 only as the next mission starts, after the shell has chosen its records; the debrief clears an unlock variable it tests while the weapon or chassis is locked. The flags the grant loop reads past its table, 43 to 49, name no weapon and are not listed. In a Present when sentence the variable reads "campaign variable 22 (ATC50 units the debrief adds to the armory)".
- **Used in this mission**: each other variable the `.MSN` touches, with what touches it ("Campaign variable 100: 2 triggers add to it, 3 records test it"): row 1's flag tests, row 10's and row 17's writes, the out-of-action reports of rows 12 to 14 and 16, and the flags row 2's header patches clear (`CampaignVariableUses`). No other mission is read.
- **Any campaign variable**: a number, 0 to 999, typed in the filter box, offered as "Campaign variable N", for one another mission sets or tests. A typed number filters the lists above to that variable alone.

For a write the debrief's list comes first, then Used in this mission, then Set by the game, each of those named by what a write to it amounts to (`CampaignVariableLabels.WriteLabel`): what replaces the value written, the debrief for 0, 8 and 9 and the next mission's load for 1, 2 and 4 to 6 ([`../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md`](../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md#the-campaign-flag-array-is-the-msn-condition-store)), and for 10 the squadmates the player has taken down in this mission, which the mission's load zeroes and a write adds to. Of the game's variables, retail missions write only 0: all 66 retail action writes add 1 to it, which posts the action's message and lasts only until the debrief sets the outcome (`HercWorks.Query values --row 10 --field CounterPairs`). Retail writes to the debrief's variables are 20 (herc, structure and group out-of-action reports and one objective), 22 to 42 (herc and group out-of-action reports, most setting 1, and one objective) and 50 to 53 and 56 to 63 (objectives, adding or subtracting 1).

### What a set writes

The clauses are a chain of row-1 records, each requiring the one before, the record naming the outermost (`PresenceRules`), written as one step:

- A variable clause reuses a flag test of the same variable, operator, value and requirement read after its requirement; otherwise a new one is added.
- The chance is a draw requiring the variables, so the roll is made only when they hold, as each retail draw requires its flag-10 test ([`../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md`](../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md#the-debriefs-loss-line)), and a range over it. A new chance is a new draw below 100 and a range from 0 to N-1 (the editor's choice, so a percent is one step; every retail draw is below 11), appended; another record's draw is never reused, since two records on one roll appear together. A chance only this record uses is rewritten in place, and a variable added to it goes in just before its draw, so no draw moves.
- Each condition of the old chain, outermost first, is then deleted once nothing references it ([Conditions](#conditions)'s Used by), a draw the change orphaned included.

The clauses cannot state, and Advanced alone sets, a range over a variant table (an option of a random choice), a range over a draw another record or range also uses (a shared roll: "It shares its roll with other records, so its chance is set under Advanced."), a chain through a GUID row 1 carries twice, a type the load does not read, a test that never holds, or a draw or range inside the chain. The view shows the sentence where it can be read, the reason, and Advanced.

A GUID can have several records, a base and the overlays or replacements that apply in other versions ([`../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md`](../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md#repeated-guids)): when they carry the same condition, a set writes every one; when they differ nothing is set ("Its details change between versions of the mission, so its presence cannot be set as one rule."), since writing one alone could leave another making a half-filled copy where the first is absent, and Advanced lists each record's condition. A variant alternative (GUID `-1`) is never written: a keyed record's condition is its own word, outside the span it copies ([`../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md`](../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md#variants)), and a record that fails its condition draws no variant.

An order another slot runs is first given its own copy. Where an order is absent its slot holds none and later orders do not move up, so a first order is refused where its group cannot be left without one (`GroupOrders.WhyNeedsFirstOrder`, the rule that refuses removing a last order), and a later one notes that the group stops at the order before it.

While a record carries a condition, the view lists what loses its reference to it where it is absent, one identity link per line, from the delete checks' lists: an object's objectives, actions and orders (`RosterDeletion.Check`) and the groups whose member slots reference it; a group's objectives, actions and orders (`RosterDeletion.GroupNamers`); the group slots running an order.

No Present when:

- the player's squad, its orders and the hercs its member slots reference, since the squad build reads group 0's members (the editor's choice);
- a route, as the user chose: every conditioned retail route record is a variant alternative, GUID `-1` (`HercWorks.Query values --row 8 --field GUID --where ConditionRef!=-1`), so every route is present in every version;
- a trigger area, a trigger and a countdown: no retail record of rows 9 to 11 carries a condition (`HercWorks.Query values --row 9 --field ConditionRef`, and rows 10 and 11), and what VSHELL does with one there is not checked.
