# Handoff — conditions, random tables and Present when in the editor

Which version of a mission the editor shows, and how it edits row 1's flag tests and draws, random tables, and which versions a record is present in. Part of the [editor handoff](handoff-editor.md). How conditions and draws resolve, and what moves the draws, is [Conditions and random tables](handoff-editor-document.md#conditions-and-random-tables); which records each tool writes is [The tools](handoff-editor-document.md#the-tools).

## What the editor shows

The editor shows every record of a mission, the same on every open, with nothing to change which campaign variables or options it shows (the user's choice): each thing by its base, the records carrying its first record's condition, and each user of a random table with the table's first option in row-1 order ([The layout](handoff-editor-document.md#the-layout)). The generation behind the open and Export draws every random value at its lowest. An open (File > Load from VOL, File > New mission) draws through `MissionOpener` from a generator whose every step is 0 (`SimRandom.Lowest`, the editor's choice): every row-1 draw at 0, so each range over a draw holding 0 holds; every random table on the option a roll of 0 picks, the first in row-1 order for each of the 252 retail tables under a key; flags 4 to 6 at 0; and a training load's new career and squad build drawn from the same zeros. The flags are the open's: in training mode (practice, `INSTANT ACTION`, a new mission, one `gam\career.dat` does not list) the array is cleared and flags 3 to 6 seeded (`ShellTrainingLaunch.SeedDrawnFlags`); at a campaign position it starts empty, as at a career with no history, the load writing the position into flags 1 and 2 and seeding 3 to 6 likewise. The game's own launches draw from `ShellTrainingLaunch.StartupRandom`.

A herc, flyer, structure, ground vehicle, group, route or trigger area some loads leave out still stands in the scene, placed from the `.MSN` by its base, and every view, an objective's, a trigger's, a countdown's and an order slot's included, shows its record in full from the layout, opening with its [Present when](#present-when) ([Selection and panels](handoff-editor-viewing.md#selection-and-panels)). The outliner lists every group, trigger area, trigger, countdown, route and objective in its place whatever its condition, and marks the row of one not in every version "(not always present)", so one a Present when took out can be selected and brought back.

## Conditions

The Conditions list, under the collapsed **Advanced** header at the end of the Mission panel, shows every row-1 record in file order as "Condition {GUID}: {test}" (`ConditionEditing.Describe`: "flag 100 > 0", "draw below 11", "draw 0 to 9 of condition 66", "random table 170 below 100", with "if condition 65" after one that requires another), each operator worded as the load tests flag against value, flag on the left. Nothing in the list creates a condition; Present when makes the flag tests and draws it needs ([What a set writes](#what-a-set-writes)), and a draw's view adds a range over it.

The view (`ConditionView`, through `ConditionEditor`) opens with "Condition {GUID>" and its kind, then:

- a flag test's flag, picked from the campaign variable list Present when uses ([The campaign variable picker](#the-campaign-variable-picker)), its operator and its value;
- a draw's bound; adding or deleting a draw, or changing whether one is made, changes every later draw and pick, and a draw's view says so;
- a range's draw (a choice of the draws before it, with a link) and its two ends, with a note when the ends lie past the draw's bound;
- Requires: none or any condition before it, with a link (a range's draw is its requirement, and it has no other). Row 1 is read in order, each record against the survivors before it, so a later one is refused with the reason and not offered;
- Used by: every record whose condition references the GUID, in rows 1 to 17 (row 5's 64-byte records by their first word) and the `.ENG`, one short line each, named from the layout as everywhere ([Names](handoff-editor-mission-logic.md#names)), a link where a view exists ("Condition 7", "Cybrid hercs (group 12)", "SCARAB in Cybrid hercs (group 5)", "2nd order of Cybrid hercs (group 4)") and plain text otherwise ("Text line 204", a herc no group places, and the text package as "The mission's objective, briefing and intelligence lines (Edit > Mission Settings)"). A record of a row the editor has no view for is named by what it does, counted, with "(not shown in the editor)" ([`../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md`](../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md)): a header patch "Mission settings, such as the zone", since it sets the header's words and clears the campaign variables it lists as the mission loads; a movie choice "A briefing or debrief movie"; another text package "Another version of the mission's lines and briefing movie"; a row-5 record "The debrief: its lines and movie"; a point "A position"; a heading "A heading". Options of random tables and orders no group runs are counted, and the templates it conditions counted apart, since no version places a template;
- Delete, refused while Used by lists anything.

No retail mission carries a row-1 GUID twice, and the view edits no condition whose GUID is. A random table, and a range over one choosing an option the table reads, open the table's [Random table view](#random-tables), the range's option highlighted; a range choosing no option the table reads is shown as any other range, read-only.

## Random tables

A type-3 condition is a **random table**, and each range over it, with the template it conditions, one of its **options**. The Mission panel lists every one after Objectives under **Random tables**, in row-1 order, by its title and how many records pick from it ("Random HERC 2: SCARAB or DIABLO (used by 3)"). Selecting one, its condition, or a range over it (from a link or the Conditions list) opens its Random table view (`RandomTableView`, through `RandomTableEditor`, read from the `.MSN` and its layout by `RandomTables`), the range's option highlighted:

- its title: what its options are, numbered from 1 among the tables of that kind in row-1 order (the editor's choice, so two tables of one chassis read apart), then its options: "Random HERC 2: SCARAB or DIABLO", each type once, three at most and then "or N more"; "Random ground vehicle 1: ...", "Random route 3: 3 options";
- emphasized, a warning when it has no effect in play ([below](#when-a-random-table-has-no-effect));
- each option on a line, numbered from 1: its chance, a percent to one decimal in a typed field; what it is, with what tells it from the others (a herc's chassis, and its weapons when another option has that chassis; a flyer's type; a structure's or ground vehicle's type and the name the MFD prints; a route's waypoint count and length, and its first waypoint when another option matches both); "(in the scene)" after the option the records picking from it show, the first ([What the editor shows](#what-the-editor-shows)); Remove;
- No option, the share left over, and what a load landing there does;
- Add option;
- **Used by**: every layout entry of rows 6 to 8 and 12 to 15 one of whose records picks by the table's key, whatever its condition, one identity link per line in game terms with the option the scene shows it with ("SCARAB 2 of 3 (group 4): shows option 1", "The player's HERC", "Squadmate 2's HERC", "Route 3", "SCARAB (no group places it)"), and the templates that pick from it themselves, counted;
- Delete random table;
- **Advanced**, collapsed: its condition number and key, its requirement with a link, its roll, each option's condition number (a link, which highlights that option) and range, and any other table under the same key, as links.

A table the editor shows only has no control and says why in plain words.

**Chances.** An option's share is the rolls its range holds that no range before it holds, out of the bound. A table opens with its shares rounded to one decimal adding up to the rounded total, largest fractions first, with a note giving them exactly where they are not (`RandomTableEditing.Percents`, `Exact`); `TRAIN5`'s table of eight options below 400 shows 12.5% each, exactly. A chance edit writes every chance as shown: the ranges contiguous from 0 in row-1 order, over the bound 100 when every chance is a whole percent and 1000 otherwise (`RandomTableEditing.ChanceBound`). A roll below any bound of 1 or more is one step of VSHELL's generator (`ShellRandom_Below`, [`../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md`](../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md#the-shells-generator)), so writing the bound moves no later pick, and no retail table rolls below less than 1. The chances add up to 100% at most. A roll in what is left over picks no option, so the table's user keeps its own fields, and the point, heading and actions those reference are not looked up, since a user's refs are resolved only in what it copies (`MissionGenerator.ReadHercs` and its siblings), as the view says for a herc, flyer, structure or ground vehicle.

**Add option** copies an option, the one the scene shows in this view and the selected one in [the Loadout section](#the-loadout-section). With n options, the new one takes the chances' total over n + 1 and each other keeps n/(n + 1) of its chance, so the share left over stays as it was. **Remove** shares the option's chance over the others in proportion to theirs, evenly when theirs are all 0, so the share left over stays as it was; it is refused for the last option. Both round each chance to one decimal keeping the total, the largest remainders rounded up first, the first of a tie first (`RandomTableEditing.AddOption`, `RemoveOption`; the editor's choices). **Delete random table** is refused while any record, in any row and whatever its condition, has the table's key (those under Used by), or anything else references the table, those listed as links.

### When a random table has no effect

A squad HERC's pick is not used where the launch's rule gives the squad other machines or leaves the HERC out ([The player's squad](handoff-editor-roster.md#the-players-squad)): in a campaign mission, whose squad pilots the career's HERCs; the player's HERC on practice rows 4 to 7, where the player pilots a stock fit of the practice screen's Herc Type; the last squadmate a training load lists, who stays behind; and a squadmate after a gap in group 0's member slots, which no squad build reads. Used by says so under that record's line, and the view warns, emphasized, when every user of the table is one of these ("No effect in play: the player pilots the Herc Type chosen on the practice screen.").

### Which tables are edited

A table is edited when each of its ranges conditions one GUID `-1` template of row 12, 13 or 14 that picks from no table itself, all of one row, no other table carries its key, and no squad HERC picks from it. Any other is shown with its reason and no control: route tables, tables under a shared key (`C1_05`'s conditions 36 and 40 share key 172, the second requiring a flag test and reached only by rolls the first leaves uncovered), and the training missions' squad tables. Of the 783 retail ranges over a table, 694 choose a herc, 30 a flyer, 12 a structure and 47 a route (`HercWorks.Query conditions`).

### The Loadout section

The view of a herc outside the squad shows, after its team, a **Loadout:** section; that of a flyer, structure or ground vehicle shows the same section as **Type:**, since its random table varies its type (`LoadoutView`). It is **Fixed** or **Random**:

- **Fixed** edits the record's own chassis and fit, or type ([Type and fit](handoff-editor-roster.md#type-and-fit)).
- Fixed to Random makes a table whose single option is a copy of what the scene shows the record as (the option its roll shows in the layout, else its own fields; `RandomTableEditing.PickOf`), so it looks the same: bound 100, one range from 0 to 99, a key no table or record carries, requiring nothing.
- **Random** lists the table's options, each a row that selects it: its number, its chance in a typed field, what it is, as the Random table view words them ([Random tables](#random-tables)), and "(in the scene)" after the option the scene shows the record with; then "No option" with its note where a share is left over; **Add option**, which copies the selected option and selects the new one; and **Remove option N** for the selected one, refused for the last. Under the list, "Editing option N:" and what that option is, then its chassis and fit, or its type ([Editing a record that picks](#editing-a-record-that-picks)).
- The selected option is the view's own, by its range, kept while the view shows the same table: the first by default, and when it is gone, as after a Remove or an undo, the one before where it stood, or the first (the editor's choice).
- When other records pick from the table, a line says that the random loadout (type) is shared with them and that editing an option changes it for all of them, then each by its identity link as Used by lists it, and templates of other tables counted.
- **Use a different table** offers each other edited table of the record's row by its title; one chosen makes the record pick from it, the table's templates read after its key record moved to just before it.
- Random to Fixed gives the key record, as its own, the span a pick of the selected option copies, so it stands as a load picking that option makes it, position, heading and actions included, and no key.
- Random to Fixed and Use a different table delete the table the record leaves only when it is still as Random made it (one option taking every roll, its template the same as the record where a pick copies it, requiring nothing) and nothing else uses it, the rule Delete random table follows (`RandomTableEditing.WhyNoDelete`). Any other table is kept, even with nothing picking from it, and is deleted from its Random table view (the editor's choice).

The switch is refused, with the reason, for a GUID two of whose records carry a key. Each change between Fixed and Random changes whether the record rolls a table, which shifts which option each later random table picks on a given load but changes no odds and nothing the editor shows, so the view does not mention it (the editor's choice).

A record whose table the editor shows only ([Which tables are edited](#which-tables-are-edited)) has no switch: in place of the section, **Random table:** with a link to the table and the option the scene shows it with, or that it shows none of them and keeps its own details, the reason, and its chassis and fit, or type, as the layout shows them, written in the record that supplies them. A squad herc that picks from a table shows the same line after its Present when, and offers no choice.

A route whose waypoints are an option says "Its waypoints are option N of" with a link to its table, and that editing them changes that option wherever it is picked.

### Editing a record that picks

A record picking from a table takes its position and its engaged and defeated actions from the option, so a move or an action set writes an overlay of its own. Its type, and a herc's weapons and guidance, are the option's: where a field comes from an edited table's option (`RandomTableEditing.WhyNoTemplateField`), the Loadout section shows it as a load picking the selected option makes it and writes it in that option's template (`RosterFit.Write`, Core, `Data/File/Msn/RosterFit.cs`), so every record picking that option changes. A pick copies the same span whichever option it picks, so the fields copied from the option the scene shows are those copied from each. `RosterFit` routes each field on its own: a field an overlay of the record sets is its own in every option, and is written in that overlay, by the squad's rules for a value an overlay cannot carry ([The player's squad](handoff-editor-roster.md#the-players-squad)); a herc view whose fields are partly the option's and partly its own says that part stays as set whichever option a load picks. A Cybrid's hardpoints are routed the same way ([A Cybrid's weapons](handoff-editor-roster.md#a-cybrids-weapons)).

Gaps:

- Only a type and a herc's weapons and guidance are written in the option; its other fields are not edited.
- A table's requirement is shown and not set; a new table requires nothing.
- A route keeps the waypoint list it picks, edited as the option every route picking it shares; route and squad tables are read-only.
- A route that picks from a table the editor does not edit shows no Random table line.

## Present when

The view of a herc, flyer, structure or ground vehicle, a group and an objective shows, after its name, which versions of the mission the record is present in, and each order slot after its other fields (`PresentWhenView`, through `PresenceEditor`). Everything is set in that view:

- a sentence, emphasized: "Present: always.", "Present only when campaign variable 30 is more than 0, and then in about 50% of those loads." (the roll is made only where the variables hold), "Present in about 50% of loads.";
- **Always**, **Only when a campaign variable...** or **In some loads...**, then a row per clause. A variable clause is a campaign variable ([the picker](#the-campaign-variable-picker)), a test (is, is not, is less than, is at most, is more than, is at least: the operators as the load tests flag against value, flag on the left) and a value, with **Remove**. The chance clause is "In about [N]% of loads", 1 to 99, with **Remove**. **And also when...** adds a variable row, written once a variable is picked, as "is more than 0" (`ConditionEditing.NewOperator` and `NewValue`); **And only in some loads...** adds the one chance, at 50% (the editor's choice). Choosing In some loads... keeps the chance and drops the variables. Adding or removing a chance adds or removes a draw, which shifts which option each later random table picks on a given load but changes no odds and nothing the editor shows, so the view does not mention it (the editor's choice);
- when the record's details change between versions, a sentence per condition naming what changes, by the record types' field decodes (`RecordAspects`): "Its chassis and weapons change when campaign variable 250 is 1.", or "Some of its details change ..." where no field it sets has a name a designer knows; then that an edit in this view does not change those details in those versions ([Which record an edit writes](handoff-editor-document.md#which-record-an-edit-writes));
- **Advanced**, collapsed: the condition its records carry, a choice of Always and the mission's flag tests and ranges over a draw named as the Conditions list names them ("Condition 5: flag 30 > 0"), with a link; each record's own condition when they differ ("Record 2: condition 5 (flag 30 > 0)"); and, for a user of a random table, that every later pick changes where it is absent. A draw, a random table and a range over one are not offered.

A change keeps the record selected. Where the mission as opened then leaves the record out, its view keeps showing its name and its Present when ([What the editor shows](#what-the-editor-shows)).

Two warnings follow the clauses, in red:

- In a mission not played in the campaign (one whose squad is not the career's, [The player's squad](handoff-editor-roster.md#the-players-squad)), a rule with a clause on any variable but the three random numbers that does not hold at 0 is never present: the launch clears every variable but those three ([What the editor shows](#what-the-editor-shows)), and the view says so.
- A herc, flyer, structure or ground vehicle whose group the mission check finds left with no members where they are absent ([Mission check](handoff-editor-viewing.md#mission-check)) says what that does, with **Move "Present when" to its group**, which writes the object's condition into the group's records and clears its own, one step (`RecordConditions.MoveToGroup`); it is refused, with the reason, unless the group is always present.

### The campaign variable picker

A dropdown (`CampaignVariablePicker`), which a flag test's view uses for its flag too, and an action's or objective's writes for theirs ([Campaign variable writes](handoff-editor-mission-logic.md#campaign-variable-writes)), with a box at its top that filters it as the user types:

- **Set by the game**, by what they hold (`CampaignVariableLabels`): 0 the outcome of the previous mission, whose clause is a choice of Won and Not won in place of a test and a value (written `> 0` and `< 1`, the tests of it retail writes most; any test holding for exactly one of 1 and 0 reads as that one), the results' outcome being whether the mission's last objective check answered success ([`../retail/simulation/mission-objectives.md`](../retail/simulation/mission-objectives.md#what-the-mission-leaves-the-shell--mission_writeresults-0042412c)); 1 the campaign chapter (a career stage: 1 to 5 for the chapters, 0 for practice) and 2 the mission index in it, the first 0 ([`../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md`](../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md#the-campaign-table--gamcareerdat)), which no retail mission tests; 4 to 6 three random numbers picked at each load, 0 to 11; 8 the squad HERCs the debrief wrote off for coming back below 30 mean condition ([`../retail/shell/armory.md`](../retail/shell/armory.md#scrapping)), not those the armory's Scrap button scraps, and 9 the squadmates killed, their condition at 0 ([`../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md`](../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md#pilot-progression)), both after the previous mission; 10 the squadmates the player took down (disabled or destroyed) in the previous mission ([`../retail/simulation/mission-deployment.md`](../retail/simulation/mission-deployment.md#the-mission-counters--dat_004a9ef4)). Flag 3 is not listed. Every other value is a number.
- **Read by the game after the mission**, by what the debrief does with each as the mission left it (`CampaignVariableLabels.Effect`), with what in this mission touches it: 20 the salvage bonus, 25 tons for each 1 ([`../retail/simulation/mission-deployment.md`](../retail/simulation/mission-deployment.md#the-mission-counters--dat_004a9ef4)); 21 to 42 the units of a weapon, by its armory name, that the debrief adds to the armory ("ATC50 units the debrief adds to the armory"); 50 to 53 and 56 to 59 a weapon the debrief unlocks when the variable holds a set value ([`../retail/formats/weapons-dat.md`](../retail/formats/weapons-dat.md#campaign-grants--armory_grantcampaignweapons-004126be)), and 60 to 63 a chassis likewise ([`../retail/formats/herc-catalogs.md`](../retail/formats/herc-catalogs.md#chassis-unlocks--herc_grantunlocks-004118c5)). Each label says what the game does with the variable, which holds for a test in a later mission too: there the variable carries what the missions before left, since the debrief does not clear 21 to 42 and the simulator zeroes 20 to 42 only as the next mission starts, after the shell has chosen its records; the debrief clears an unlock variable it tests while the weapon or chassis is locked. The flags the grant loop reads past its table, 43 to 49, name no weapon and are not listed. In a Present when sentence the variable reads "campaign variable 22 (ATC50 units the debrief adds to the armory)".
- **Used in this mission**: each other variable the `.MSN` touches, with what touches it ("Campaign variable 100: 2 triggers add to it, 3 records test it"): row 1's flag tests, row 10's and row 17's writes, the out-of-action reports of rows 12 to 14 and 16, and the flags row 2's header patches clear (`CampaignVariableUses`). No other mission is read.
- **Any campaign variable**: a number, 0 to 999, typed in the filter box, offered as "Campaign variable N", for one another mission sets or tests. A typed number filters the lists above to that variable alone.

For a write the debrief's list comes first, then Used in this mission, then Set by the game, each of those named by what a write to it amounts to (`CampaignVariableLabels.WriteLabel`): what replaces the value written, the debrief for 0, 8 and 9 and the next mission's load for 1, 2 and 4 to 6 ([`../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md`](../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md#the-campaign-flag-array-is-the-msn-condition-store)), and for 10 the squadmates the player has taken down in this mission, which the mission's load zeroes and a write adds to. Of the game's variables, retail missions write only 0: all 66 retail action writes add 1 to it, which posts the action's message and lasts only until the debrief sets the outcome (`HercWorks.Query values --row 10 --field CounterPairs`). Retail writes to the debrief's variables are 20 (herc, structure and group out-of-action reports and one objective), 22 to 42 (herc and group out-of-action reports, most setting 1, and one objective) and 50 to 53 and 56 to 63 (objectives, adding or subtracting 1).

### What a set writes

The clauses are a chain of row-1 records, each requiring the one before, the record naming the outermost (`PresenceRules`), written as one step:

- A variable clause reuses a flag test of the same variable, operator, value and requirement read after its requirement; otherwise a new one is added.
- The chance is a draw requiring the variables, so the roll is made only when they hold, as each retail draw requires its flag-10 test ([`../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md`](../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md#the-debriefs-loss-line)), and a range over it. A new chance is a new draw below 100 and a range from 0 to N-1 (the editor's choice, so a percent is one step; every retail draw is below 11), appended; another record's draw is never reused, since two records on one roll appear together. A chance only this record uses is rewritten in place, and a variable added to it goes in just before its draw, so no draw moves.
- Each condition of the old chain, outermost first, is then deleted once nothing references it ([Conditions](#conditions)'s Used by), a draw the change orphaned included.

The clauses cannot state, and Advanced alone sets, a range over a random table (one of its options), a range over a draw another record or range also uses (a shared roll: "It shares its roll with other records, so its chance is set under Advanced."), a chain through a GUID row 1 carries twice, a type the load does not read, a test that never holds, or a draw or range inside the chain. The view shows the sentence where it can be read, the reason, and Advanced.

A GUID can have several records, a base and the overlays or replacements that apply in other versions ([`../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md`](../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md#repeated-guids)): when they carry the same condition, a set writes every one; when they differ nothing is set ("Its details change between versions of the mission, so its presence cannot be set as one rule."), since writing one alone could leave another making a half-filled copy where the first is absent, and Advanced lists each record's condition. A template (GUID `-1`) is never written: a user's condition is its own word, outside the span it copies ([`../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md`](../retail/formats/msn-mission-file.md#random-tables)), and a record that fails its condition picks no option.

An order another slot runs is first given its own copy. Where an order is absent its slot holds none and later orders do not move up, so a first order is refused where its group cannot be left without one (`GroupOrders.WhyNeedsFirstOrder`, the rule that refuses removing a last order), and a later one notes that the group stops at the order before it.

While a record carries a condition, the view lists what loses its reference to it where it is absent, one identity link per line, from the delete checks' lists: an object's objectives, actions and orders (`RosterDeletion.Check`) and the groups whose member slots reference it; a group's objectives, actions and orders (`RosterDeletion.GroupNamers`); the group slots running an order.

No Present when:

- the player's squad, its orders and the hercs its member slots reference, since the squad build reads group 0's members (the editor's choice);
- a route, as the user chose: every conditioned retail route record is a template, GUID `-1` (`HercWorks.Query values --row 8 --field GUID --where ConditionRef!=-1`), so every route is present in every version;
- a trigger area, a trigger and a countdown: no retail record of rows 9 to 11 carries a condition (`HercWorks.Query values --row 9 --field ConditionRef`, and rows 10 and 11), and what VSHELL does with one there is not checked.

## Open

- **Open:** a variation is named in its record's "Present when" and not edited there; the user's choice is to list each variation in the view with its fields editable ("When campaign variable 30 is more than 0: type APOCA, weapons ..."), an edit inside it writing that record.
