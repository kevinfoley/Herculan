# Handoff — hercs, flyers, ground vehicles, structures and the squad in the editor

Adding roster objects from the Toolbox, changing their type and fit, a group's side, deleting, and the player's squad. Part of the [editor handoff](handoff-editor.md). Which records each tool writes, and the retail counts behind each new record's fields, are in [The tools](handoff-editor-document.md#the-tools).

## The Toolbox

The Toolbox (`EditorToolbox`), docked in the bottom half of the left side under the Mission panel, has one tab each for Routes, Trigger Areas, Hercs (the 21 mech types of `nam\MECHS.NAM`), Flyers, Ground Vehicles, Structures and Bases ([Bases](#bases)); a placing mode started elsewhere brings its tab up. Each roster tab but Bases lists its types under the name the MFD status screen prints, with a Team choice (Human or Cybrid), and its Add takes only an entry chosen in the tab shown. An entry is added by dragging it out and letting go over the view (on the ground under the pointer), by double-clicking it, or with Add (on the ground at the middle of the view, or under the camera when that is sky, which the gamepad's pointer reaches too). While dragged over the view, the object it would add stands on the ground under the pointer (`DropPreview`, drawn but not picked, listed or levelled under). Letting go over a panel adds nothing. After an add the scene is synced with the document, which builds the object through `MissionScene.CreateObject`, the path every object of the editor's is built by (its model uploaded then when nothing else draws with it), levels the ground under it, and selects it by its record. Undo takes the records out; Redo puts the same ones back under the same GUIDs, with the same object standing for them.

### Joining and caps

With a group or one of its members selected, **Join the selected group** adds to that group instead; the Bases tab has no such choice, since a base is always a group of its own. While the group cannot take the entry chosen or dragged, the checkbox is disabled and unticked with the reason under it, by the check the add itself refuses by (each placer's `WhyNotJoin`, through `HercGroups`, `FlyerGroups` and `VehicleGroups`), and the add gets a group of its own; an entry double-clicked in the frame it is chosen is checked again and refused with the reason when the box was ticked. The box starts unticked and is ticked for one group: selecting another group, one of its members included, shows it unticked, so an add joins only the group it was ticked for. A joined member takes the group's team.

An add past DBSIM's slots for its block is refused and that Toolbox tab's entries disabled with the reason above them ("At maximum HERC limit (100)."), counted as the mission check counts them, over every version of the mission at once (`RosterLimits`): every GUID of the roster's row whatever its condition, and for block 7 one machine per squad position group 0 gives, which `player.mec` brings. Block 7 holds 100 hercs, block 8 50 flyers and block 9 140 structures and ground vehicles together. A base takes a block-9 slot per member, so an entry of the Bases tab that would pass the cap is disabled on its own, with the reason in its tooltip and under Add while it is chosen.

## Structures

The 45 structure types of `BASES.DAT`; the three invulnerable ones, 21 to 23 ([`../retail/formats/bases-dat.md`](../retail/formats/bases-dat.md)), carry ", can't be destroyed" after their name in the Toolbox and in a structure's Type choice, and such a structure's view says it takes no damage. `StructurePlacer` adds one in a group of its own, facing heading 0; the mission check passes it, since a group naming a heading needs no slot-0 order. Joined to a structure group, it names a point of its own where it was put and faces the group's way.

## Bases

The Bases tab offers eleven ready-made bases, one per `dat\BFORMS.DAT` formation with a pad layout (`BaseTemplates`). Their names are the editor's own, since no retail file names a formation, and a note under the list says so. The retail missions use each pad formation as a fixed template; each base holds the building list retail places in its formation most often, by member slot (`HercWorks.Query formations`), ground vehicles included where retail has them. An entry's tooltip lists what it holds, its structures and then its ground vehicles, each by its Toolbox name with how many.

`StructurePlacer.AddBase` adds the whole base as one structure group, on the Team chosen, in one step of the undo history, and selects the group. The group lays its base pad, so the drop point only picks the ground tile and the group stands at the formation's fixed spot in it, the tile painted and levelled ([`../retail/formats/script-dat.md`](../retail/formats/script-dat.md#base-formation-terrain)). It faces heading 0, since the pad is painted on a tile aligned with the map while the members' formation spots turn with the heading. No member names a point or heading of its own, so each stands on its slot's spot, turned by its slot ([rule 7](../retail/formats/script-dat.md#placement--the-actual-rule)). While a base is dragged over the view the preview draws every member where it will stand, jumping a whole tile at a time; it leaves the ground as it is, the pad and the levelling coming with the add. A type the install builds no object for puts every record back and names the type.

## Hercs

`HercPlacer` adds a herc in a new group along a one-waypoint route, standing on that waypoint facing heading 0. The group's first order is by its team (`NewGroups.FirstOrder`, the editor's choice), as the Hercs note in the Toolbox says: a Cybrid group hunts the player's squad (search/destroy on group 0); a Human group patrols, naming no target, since search/destroy fights only its target ([`../retail/simulation/ai-navigation.md`](../retail/simulation/ai-navigation.md#searchdestroy-12--mech_behavioursearchdestroythink-0041d60c)) and the AI never targets its own side ([`../retail/simulation/ai-targeting.md`](../retail/simulation/ai-targeting.md#is-it-a-target-at-all--ai_istargetable-00411e80)), so on the squad it would never fight, while a patrolling leader takes any enemy it finds. Retail Human herc groups outside the squad have no majority first order (of 21 records, 7 guard a group, 6 sleep, 3 patrol naming no target); the drag preview stands at its ride height, a RAZOR 5,000 units higher, where it starts. Its fit: a Human chassis (types 0 to 8) takes its stock fit from `gam\ini_*.dat`; a Cybrid (types 9 to 20), which no shell file fits and every mission fits in its own records, often through a random table's templates, takes the fit most retail records of its type carry, the first in mission and record order on a tie (the editor's choice). A join takes the first free member slot up to slot 7, the last `dat\MFORMS.DAT` spreads (the editor's limit), and stands in its formation place. Group 0 is refused; its view adds squadmates. Deleting the herc deletes its group and order; the route stays and can then be deleted.

## Flyers

The types of `nam\FLYERS.NAM` the install has a `dat\<type>.DAT` for (`SKIMMER` as LANDSKIMMER); `HOVTANK` and `DROPSHIP`, which have no data to fly or be shot with ([`../retail/simulation/ai-flyers.md`](../retail/simulation/ai-flyers.md)), are named in a note and not offered. `FlyerPlacer` adds one as a herc is added, a Human flight patrolling its route and attacking any HERC or structure it finds ([`../retail/simulation/ai-flyers.md`](../retail/simulation/ai-flyers.md#the-thinks)). Its waypoint has Z 0, so DBSIM starts it at 5,000 units ([`../retail/formats/script-dat.md`](../retail/formats/script-dat.md#placement--the-actual-rule), rule 5), as every retail flyer group starts, and the drag preview stands there; the group's Z handle changes it after. A join takes the first free slot up to slot 3, the last `dat\FFORMS.DAT` places beside the leader (the editor's limit; no retail flyer group has more than two members). Deleting the flyer deletes its group and order.

## Ground vehicles

The 15 types of `BASES.DAT` that DBSIM builds as ground vehicles ([`../retail/simulation/structure-behaviour.md`](../retail/simulation/structure-behaviour.md#five-classes-one-switch)); the other five vehicle types, for which it builds no object, are named in a note and not offered. `VehiclePlacer` adds one as a convoy, a structure group whose first order is travel. With one waypoint the leader has nowhere to drive ([`../retail/simulation/ground-vehicles.md`](../retail/simulation/ground-vehicles.md#the-ground-vehicle-tick--0046a5d0)), so it stands until waypoints are added to its route, and the Toolbox says so.

A structure group whose first member is a ground vehicle is a convoy. A vehicle joins one in the first free slot up to slot 3, since the leader pick, `Group_FirstAbleMember` (`0046a4b8`), reaches only the first four members (the editor's limit), naming no point, so it takes its formation post, as 29 of the 30 retail convoy members past slot 0 do. Joined to any other structure group it is placed as a structure is and stands there as a building, as the vehicles of the 39 retail base groups led by a building do; the 13 retail base groups with no orders that a vehicle leads are convoys to the editor. A structure joins a convoy as any structure group. Deleting the vehicle deletes its convoy and order.

## Moving to another group

An object's view outside the squad offers "Move to another group...": every group of its roster but its own and the squad's, by name, each it cannot move into disabled with the reason on hover (`MemberMover`, through Core's `MemberMoves`). The group taking it is checked as a join is ([Joining and caps](#joining-and-caps)), a ground vehicle moving into a convoy as a vehicle joins one, and the object goes in the first member slot no record of the group fills. Its GUID leaves every member slot of its old group; a group it leaves with no member goes with it, as on a delete ([Deleting](#deleting)), and the move is refused while anything references that group. A herc or flyer naming no point of its own, and a ground vehicle moving into a convoy, then stand in their formation place; a structure naming none, or a vehicle moving into a group that is not a convoy, is given a point where it stands, as a joined structure is. It takes the new group's team, orders and Present when. An object more than one group places is refused. One step of the undo history.

## Type and fit

Outside the squad:

- **A structure's or ground vehicle's Type** offers the Toolbox's structures, or ground vehicles, so a building never becomes a vehicle or the reverse, and a convoy stays a convoy (`RosterTypeEditor`, the editor's choice). The ground is levelled under the new object.
- **A flyer's Type** offers the Toolbox's flyers.
- **A herc's Chassis** offers the 21 types; the new type takes the fit an add gives it, all ten slots written. Then one row per hardpoint, a weapon and for a launcher a guidance (`MachineFitter`, drawn by `MachineFitView`): a Human chassis's offered and written as the squad's ([The player's squad](#the-players-squad)), a Cybrid's as [A Cybrid's weapons](#a-cybrids-weapons) says.

A field a random table supplies is its template's to edit ([Random tables](handoff-editor-conditions.md#random-tables)). Each change builds a new object and keeps it selected; the mission check tests no roster record's type, so it finds nothing new. A flyer's, structure's or ground vehicle's Type, and a herc's Chassis and hardpoints, are changed for any record the layout has (`RosterTypeEditor`, `MachineFitter`), one the mission as opened leaves out included.

### A Cybrid's weapons

No shell file says which weapons a Cybrid's mounts take, so by the user's choice the Editor Settings' "Cybrid Loadouts" picks what they offer (`CybridWeapons.Offered`). Vanilla, the default, offers what retail missions fit (`CybridWeapons`, over Core's `RetailMountFits`):

- Its hardpoints are the fit slots its `gl\<NAME>.GL` hardpoints address ([`../retail/formats/gun-layout-gl.md`](../retail/formats/gun-layout-gl.md#record)), each numbered from 1 by its slot, as the squad's are (the editor's choice: no retail screen shows a Cybrid's).
- Each offers, in a dropdown, the weapons the install's retail missions fit in that slot of that chassis: every row-12 record of the type in the missions as the archives hold them, not a mission saved over one, templates (GUID `-1`) included and no condition tested; "(empty)" only where such a record leaves the slot empty. A record that picks from a random table carries type `-1` and no fit of its own in every retail mission, and so does every later record of a GUID, so each fit counts once. The list is read once per editor run, the first time a Cybrid's hardpoints are drawn; the weapons screen's order, a weapon it does not list after by id, and "(empty)" last (the editor's choice).
- Every slot a retail record fits on a Cybrid is one its hardpoints address. MIRIMAC's sixth hardpoint is fitted by no retail record, and SPIDER has no hardpoint; such a hardpoint's weapon is read-only, with the reason.
- A weapon chosen takes the ammunition type its records carry with it in that slot most often, the lowest on a tie (the editor's choice). In retail that is the filler 5 for every weapon that takes no guidance but BMSL, which both PITBULL records carrying it give 0; a missile rack takes what its records give it there (DIABLO's MSL10 carries 5 in 125 records and ARH in 39, so it takes 5, which DBSIM reads as SARH). A launcher's guidance then stays editable, as a Human's is.
- A record holding a weapon no retail record fits there shows it as the current choice, marked "(not from a retail mission)".
- With no mission in the install's archives, every hardpoint's weapon is read-only, with a note; the guidance stays editable.

Unrestricted offers on every hardpoint of every chassis but PITBULL the weapons screen's whole list, which holds every weapon a retail Cybrid carries but BEMP and BMSL; on PITBULL's, BEMP, BPBW and BMSL alone, which no other chassis is offered (the user's choice). Retail fits BEMP and BMSL on PITBULL alone, and BPBW on no record. "(empty)" is last on both. A hardpoint no retail record fits then offers them too, and the note naming retail's fits is not shown. A weapon chosen takes its ammunition type as under Vanilla, the filler 5 where no retail record fits it there. A record holding a weapon its hardpoint does not offer marks it "(not offered here)".

A new Cybrid's fit, which `HercPlacer` and a chassis change give, is still [the most common retail fit of its type](#hercs).

### Loadout presets

A preset is a named weapon fit for one chassis, the editor's own data and invention, not a mission's (`LoadoutPresets`, drawn by `LoadoutPresetView`). The presets are kept in `loadout-presets.json` in the user's settings folder beside the editor's settings, read once per editor run and written whole on every save, overwrite or delete; a missing or unreadable file means none. Each belongs to a `nam\MECHS.NAM` type, which the squad's chassis 0 to 8 share, Human or Cybrid alike, so a MONGOOSE's "EMP" and an OUTLAW's "EMP" are two presets. A name is trimmed, and two names are the same when they match ignoring case, within one chassis. A preset holds the weapon and ammunition type of each fit slot whose weapon the view edited when it was saved.

- "Loadout preset", under Chassis, lists the chassis's presets by name and shows the one whose every slot holds its weapon and ammunition type in the HERC's fit as it stands, else "(none)"; with none saved for the chassis it is disabled and reads "(none saved)". Choosing one writes its slots as one edit and one undo step, through the same writes as a weapon choice, so while an option of a random loadout is edited it lands in that option's template where a template supplies the field. A slot whose weapon the view does not edit is left as it is.
- A preset holding a weapon its hardpoint does not offer as the hardpoints offer them now ([A Cybrid's weapons](#a-cybrids-weapons), and on a Human chassis its arming layout), or a slot that is not one of the chassis's hardpoints, is listed disabled, not hidden: a Cybrid preset saved under Unrestricted while Vanilla is chosen says it needs Unrestricted, any other that its weapons are not offered on the chassis's hardpoints (the user's choice).
- "Save preset", under the hardpoints, asks for a name and saves the HERC's fit under it, asking before it overwrites a name the chassis already has; it is disabled on a chassis whose weapons the view edits on no hardpoint. "Delete preset" deletes the preset the dropdown shows, after asking, and is disabled while it shows none. Esc in any of these prompts is its Cancel.

## Sides

A group's view offers its Team, the group's side (block 11 `0x6e`), for every group but the squad; all its members change, and the view says how many when there are others. An object's view shows its group's team fixed. `SideChanger` takes the group by its row-16 GUID, any group the layout has, and writes it; the sync then sets each member's simulation object from the layout.

## Deleting

An object's view has Delete, and the Delete key deletes the selected object. `ObjectDeleter` removes every record of the object's GUID and its group's member slots naming it; a group left with no member goes too, with its orders that no other group runs, and the view names the group that goes with the object. The roster shrinks, so the slot caps count only what can spawn. The templates a deleted record picked from stay, and deleting a random table's user moves every later roll ([Conditions and random tables](handoff-editor-document.md#conditions-and-random-tables)).

A GUID with no record resolves to `-1` (`MissionGenerator.cs:473-496`), so the delete is refused while an objective, an action or an order names the object, or the group the delete would remove, and while the object fires an action of its own when engaged or defeated, a template it can pick included. The view lists each by its identity in the layout, one the mission as opened leaves out included (`ObjectDeleter.Resolve`): an objective or trigger by its link, a trigger it fires with ": fired by it", an order as its group and slot ("Cybrid hercs (group 4): 1st order"), and the options of random tables and the orders no group runs as counts. The player's squad is refused (group 0's view removes squadmates); Core's own refusal finds the squad as the layout's first group (`RosterDeletion.Check`, `SquadRoster.Squad`). The ground is levelled again as after a move.

A group's view has "Delete group", and the Delete key deletes the selected group (`GroupDeleter`, `RosterDeletion.DeleteGroup`): every record of the group's GUID, its orders that no other group runs, and every member no other group places, as one undo step; a member another group also places stays, listed under "Stays, as another group also places it:". A base's pad goes with its group, since the group's own flag lays it. The delete is refused for the player's squad, while an objective, an action or an order names the group, and while anything names or needs a member that goes with it, each such member listed by its link with what references it. The button's tooltip says how many members go and whether a pad does.

## The player's squad

The squad is group 0, the layout's first group, read by GUID (`SquadRoster`, `SquadEditor`). Its view shows the player's HERC, its first member, then each squadmate, "Squadmate 1" to "Squadmate 3" by place in the list: group 0's members in the unbroken run from its second slot, which the squad build reads (`Squad_SetPositionsInPlay`, [`../retail/shell/main-menu.md`](../retail/shell/main-menu.md#starting-a-practice-mission)), in that order. The player's own object's view shows the player's HERC, and a squadmate's object that squadmate; a herc after a gap in the member slots, which no squad build reads, says it is not in the squad in play. No text in these views says "group 0" or "member slot".

First they say which Hercs the squad pilots in this mission, by the launch's rule (`MissionSquad`, handed out by `MissionFileLaunch.Open` and `OpenNew` from `ShellTrainingLaunch.PilotsMissionMachine`):

- On practice rows 0 to 3 and in every mission loaded as `INSTANT ACTION` (one `gam\career.dat` does not list, and a new mission), the player pilots group 0's own machine; on rows 4 to 7 a stock fit of the practice screen's Herc Type, option `0x28` in `DATA\prefs.cfg`. On all of these the squadmates pilot group 0's further machines, and the view says that the last squadmate listed stays behind and how many go: the retail bug in that doc's squad build, which this engine keeps.
- In a campaign mission the squad pilots the career's machines from `DATA\player.mec`, and group 0's squadmates set only how many squad positions are in play ([`../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md`](../retail/shell/campaign-loop.md#loading-the-careers-mission)); the view says in bold that the HERCs and weapons set here are not used and that the number of squadmates listed sets how many of the career's squadmates can go, and puts each HERC's chassis and hardpoints under a collapsed "HERC and weapons (not used in the campaign)" header. Add and Remove stay outside it, since the count is used.

Then each machine is edited in every `.MSN` mission: a chassis among the nine Herc Type steps through, and one row per mount the chassis has (`Herc_CapacityForType`), "Hardpoint 1" up (the user's spelling; the weapons screen's stepper reads "Hard Points", [`../retail/shell/weapons-and-repair.md`](../retail/shell/weapons-and-repair.md#the-weapons-screen)), numbered as the repair screen numbers them, from 1 by slot ([A row is four text columns](../retail/shell/weapons-and-repair.md#a-row-is-four-text-columns)), an empty one "(empty)", each offering the weapons of the weapons screen's list whose chassis layout (`gam\arm_*.dat`, `ShellArmingLayouts`) has a record for that socket, and for a missile rack or the Razor's launcher the four guidance kinds ([`../retail/shell/weapons-and-repair.md`](../retail/shell/weapons-and-repair.md#fitting-a-weapon)). The career's unlock flags and the armory's stock are not applied, as a mission's machine is fitted from no stock. A weapon chosen takes the guidance the weapons screen fits it with: ARH on a missile rack, none otherwise. A chassis chosen comes with its stock fit from `gam\ini_*.dat` ([`../retail/formats/herc-catalogs.md`](../retail/formats/herc-catalogs.md#gamini_dat--the-stock-fit-per-chassis)), an empty mount `0` and each slot past the capacity `-1` with filler 5, as the retail squad records have them. The player's machines of `TRAIN1` to `TRAIN4` and `TRAIN5`'s squad records carry a random table key, so their edits take overlay records.

Group 0's view adds a squadmate after the last and removes any (`SquadEditor`). A new one is of the player's HERC's chassis as it stands, with that chassis's stock fit. A remove moves each later squadmate up a slot, so order matters and the view says so. An add is refused past three squadmates, the three squad members the player structure points at and the squad build fits ([`../retail/shell/squad-and-crew.md`](../retail/shell/squad-and-crew.md#the-rows)), and past block 7's slots, the add taking a row-12 GUID and a squad position; a remove is refused as a delete is. Export builds `player.mec` from the edited mission as the launch would ([The generation](handoff-editor-document.md#the-generation)). The scene shows group 0's hercs from the `.MSN` in every mission, one object per herc its member slots reference, spread from group 0 as the squad spawns (the user's choice): the squadmate a practice load leaves behind and a herc the Herc Type option replaces are drawn too, and the views' notes explain them ([Rebuilding after an edit](handoff-editor-document.md#rebuilding-after-an-edit)). Core finds the squad as the layout's first group (`SquadRoster.Squad`).

The squad has no wiring (none of the 138 retail squad records sets a waited-on action, and none of the 278 records the squads reference sets an engaged or defeated action; DBSIM builds the machines from `player.mec`), no Present when, and no choice of random table: a squad herc that picks from one shows it, read-only (the editor's choices).

Gaps:

- An overlay cannot carry a field's unset value, so a field a template supplies keeps the template's value where an edit would unset it: after a chassis change on `TRAIN1` to `TRAIN4`, a slot past the new capacity keeps the template's weapon, and a mount that takes no guidance its ammunition type, neither of which the squad build or DBSIM reads.
- A launcher mount whose stock fit carries the filler 5 is written SARH (0), which DBSIM reads a 5 as ([`../retail/simulation/weapon-mounts.md`](../retail/simulation/weapon-mounts.md#the-join--mechloadout_constructweaponmounts-0040fff8)): the editor's choice, which leaves 0 in the generated record and `player.mec` where an unedited stock fit's launch holds 5. A herc outside the squad whose random table the editor shows only follows the same two rules.
- Removing a squadmate whose record picks from a random table, as `TRAIN5`'s do, moves every later roll.

## Gaps

- Once added, an object changes only its point, group, type (and a herc's fit), the actions it fires, the states it is present in and the table it draws from; its group changes only its team, orders and, for a flyer group, height. Nothing moves an object out into a new group of its own. The rest is in [What is left, by row](handoff-editor.md#what-is-left-by-row).
- Nothing deletes a group directly, only with its last member. A refused delete cannot be forced, and the view offers no way to clear the reference that refuses it.
