# DBSIM.EXE and GO.EXE — loading the sound drivers

**The sound manager opens the HMI library's MIDI and digital drivers and loads its banks at start-up.** Addresses are `ES1DBSIM` image addresses unless `ES1GO` is named ([the X-32 executable](../formats/x32-executable.md#the-image)). The manager is [`sound-manager.md`](sound-manager.md); what the devices do is [`drum-device.md`](drum-device.md), [`digital-mixer.md`](digital-mixer.md) and [`song-player.md`](song-player.md).

## The driver

**The sound settings come from the install's configuration, except the rate, which the image fixes at 11,025 Hz.** `Sound_Init` (`0004db14`; `es2_xref` finds its one call, from `Sim_StartMission`) takes six values in stack order — MIDI port, port, DMA channel, IRQ, MIDI device and digital device. It first sets the number of voice-line slots `000bd73c`, 10, or 1 in the low-memory mode, and makes the slots with `__vec_new` and `VoiceSlot_ElementCtor` and their 100-byte script buffers ([the speech slot](../formats/snc-lip-sync.md#the-speech-slot)); then, with the digital device 0, it makes no manager and goes to its last store, so the slots and buffers exist with no sound manager ([voice lines](../simulation/sound-catalog.md#voice-lines) says what a line does then). It stores them over the defaults block at `000959ec`, whose image values are a buffer of `0x1000`, the rate 11,025, `0x78`, DMA 1, IRQ 7, port `0x220`, digital device `0xe000`, MIDI port `0x388` and MIDI device `0xa002`. The buffer and the rate are not among the values it stores, and `es2_xref` finds one reference to the rate's dword `000959f0`, the read in `SoundManager_LoadDrivers`. The shell's block, `SoundDriver_Defaults` (`ES1GO 00082310`), holds the same values in the same layout, which `ShellSound_Init` fills from `resource.cfg` itself ([the shell's sound manager](../shell/sound.md)).

`Sound_Init` then makes the manager, runs `SoundManager_LoadDrivers` (`0004ef8c`) without reading its answer, so a failed load still goes on to the rest, sets the memory limit through `00050008` (`0xffffffff`, or 200,000 with `+0x503` set in the low-memory mode), calls `0004ffe0`(manager, 100,000), zeroes `SoundCatalog_Count` (`000bd72c`) and loads the catalog; it sets the slot count again there, to the same value. Its last store, with or without a manager, is 10 into `Sound_ListenerCountdown` (`000959b4`).

`SoundManager_LoadDrivers` (`0004ef8c`):

1. reads the three bank files whole with `SoundManager_LoadBanks` (`0004f6d4`), and fails when one does not load;
2. registers `SoundManager_TimerCallback` (`0004e710`) with `Timer_AddCallback` and keeps the slot at `+0x134`, then starts the HMI library's digital and MIDI halves with `HmiDigi_InitSystem` (`0006fdbb`) and `HmiMidi_InitSystem` (`00070ed8`), each with `sos\` ([the devices in the program](drum-device.md#the-devices-in-the-program));
3. opens the MIDI device with `HmiMidi_InitDriver` (`00071076`), handle `000bd8c0`, and loads the melodic and drum banks into it through `00072275`;
4. fills the digital driver, hardware and init blocks above from the defaults block, sets `000bd8c8` to 0, calls `HmiDigi_InitSystem` again, and opens MIDI device `0xa005` with `HmiMidi_InitDriver` on the same port block and the block at `+0xa0`, handle `000bd8c4`, loading `sos\drum32.dig` into it; that open opens the digital driver, as driver 0, the `000bd8c8` the sound effects play through ([opening](drum-device.md#opening));
5. turns the channel-stealing mode off (`HmiMidi_SetChannelStealing`, `0007225a`, with 0), sets `SoundManager_DriversLoaded` (`000bd8cc`) and returns 1.

The MIDI device's open comes before the digital driver's blocks are filled and device `0xa005` opened, so a MIDI device that fails to open leaves the digital driver unopened too ([Open](#open)). A failure formats `SoundManager::loadDrivers: %s` with `0006f23d`'s text for the error into a stack buffer that no call is given, so it is silent. From the bank loads on it closes MIDI handles with `HmiMidi_CloseDriver` (`0007144b`)(handle, 1): both after the melodic bank, `000bd8c4` before device `0xa005` has opened; `000bd8c0` alone after the drum bank and after `0xa005`'s open; both after `sos\drum32.dig`. It then calls `0006fe28`, `00071064` and `0006d5c2`(0) and returns 0. `HmiMidi_InitDriver` hands device `0xa005`'s driver the block's `+0x18` far pointer, the manager's address, and through it the digital driver, hardware and init blocks.

**`SoundManager_UnloadDrivers` (`0004f3d4`)**, (manager), the reverse, while `SoundManager_DriversLoaded` is set: stops every object through its slot `+0x14`, passes both MIDI handles (`000bd8c0`, `000bd8c4`) to `00071515` and then each with 1 to `0007144b`, removes the timer callback `+0x134` (its test against -1 compares the word zero-extended, so it always passes), calls `HmiMidi_UninitSystem` (`00071064`), `HmiDigi_UninitDriver` (`000705c5`)(`000bd8c8`, 1, 1), which closes the digital driver that device `0xa005`'s close leaves open ([closing](drum-device.md#closing-and-resetting)), and `HmiDigi_UninitSystem` (`0006fe28`), and clears it. `es2_xref` finds calls from `Sound_Shutdown` and `SoundManager_Dtor`. **`SoundManager_Dtor` (`0004eccc`)**, (manager, flags): formats `SoundManager::~SoundManager: max memory used = %d (limit %d)` with `+0x4fa` and `+0x4fe` into a stack buffer, unloads the drivers, destroys each object through its slot `+0x08` with 3, closes the volume stream `+0x504` and frees the manager on bit 0. `es2_xref` finds no caller in `ES1DBSIM`, whose `Sound_Shutdown` (`0004de08`) destroys the catalog's objects through their slot `+0x08` with 3 and unloads the drivers itself, keeping the freed objects' pointers in `SoundCatalog_Objects` (`000bd63c`) and the manager in `Sound_Manager` (`000959ac`); it does not destroy the voice-line slots' objects, which the unload stops; the shell's copy (`ES1GO 000420fd`) is called by `Shell_AssertCleanupHook` and `Shell_ShutdownDevices`.

**Finding the card.** **`SoundManager_DetectDigitalCard` (`0004ed68`)**, (manager, device), answers 1 when the HMI library's detection finds a digital card and copies what it found over the defaults block; `es2_xref` finds no caller in either image (`ES1GO 000421a7`). It works on `SoundDetect_Buffer` (`000bd742`):

1. `SoundDetect_Init` (`0004ee30`)(manager, `.\`, buffer) copies the path into the buffer and clears its bytes `+0x100` and `+0x101`.
2. With *device* 0, `SoundDetect_FindFirst` (`0004eef4`)(manager, buffer): `0007235d`(buffer), then `00072901`(buffer + `0x102`, buffer + `0x16e`), then `00072ce9`(buffer + `0x16e`); when all three return 0 it sets `+0x100` and `+0x101` and answers 1, else calls `00072517` and answers 0. `SoundDetect_Close` (`0004ef68`) then calls `00072517` when `+0x100` is set and clears it.
3. Otherwise `SoundDetect_FindDevice` (`0004ee78`)(manager, device, buffer): `0007235d`(buffer), then `00072573`(device, buffer + `0x102`, buffer + `0x16e`), then `00072ce9`(buffer + `0x16e`); it calls `00072517` either way, clears `+0x100`, and sets `+0x101` when all three returned 0, answering `+0x101`.
4. On success it copies the buffer's `+0x176` to the DMA channel `000959f8`, `+0x172` to the IRQ `000959fc`, `+0x16e` to the port `00095a00` and `+0x166` to the digital device `00095a04`.

`[SoundFX]` `Driver#` holds one of the digital devices the setup program `SOUNDSET.EXE` lists in its `SOUNDSET.INI`, up to four a card in the columns 8-bit mono, 8-bit stereo, 16-bit mono and 16-bit stereo: Sound Blaster `e000` and `e001`, Sound Blaster 16 `e015`–`e018`, and so on for 18 cards; the image's default `0xe000` is the Sound Blaster's 8-bit mono. The drivers are the 95 `.com` images in `SOS\HMIDRV.386`, a name both images hold; `HmiDigi_InitDriver` loads one with `HmiDigi_LoadDriverImage` (`00080dd1`, [loading an image](../formats/hmi-driver-container.md#loading-an-image)), the device's image whose flags have bit `0x4000`: `sb168m.com` for `e015` and `sb168s.com` for `e016` ([opening](drum-device.md#opening)). The MIDI devices are [`song-player.md`](song-player.md#the-devices)'s.

## The digital driver's timer

**The HMI code runs its timed work from the game's timer, on every interrupt: `SoundManager_LoadDrivers`' callback `SoundManager_TimerCallback` (`0004e710`) far-calls the library's dispatcher ([the library's timer](song-player.md#the-librarys-timer)).** The library can also hand the game a far callback of its own through `HmiTimer_Install`, which the game would register at a count of a sixtieth of the timer's rate; `SoundManager_LoadDrivers` starts the library's timer with the flag that skips that, in both programs, and `es2_xref.py` finds no other call of the library's start. What a count means to the timer is [Open](../simulation/debug-monitor.md#open). The digital driver's own handler is one of the library's timer events, which device `0xa005`'s open registers at 120 calls a second ([opening](drum-device.md#opening)). The functions at `00050d38`–`00050e1f`, after the sound files and before [the profile file](../formats/profile-files.md), are what the HMI code calls for it:

| Function | Address | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `HmiTimer_Install` | `00050d98` | (*a*, offset, selector), C convention: keeps the far pointer selector:offset in `HmiTimer_Callback` (`000b1ee0`) and registers `HmiTimer_Tick` with `Timer_AddCallback` (`0006d289`) at a count of ⌊⌊1000000 / `Math_Q14Multiply`(*w*, `0x35a1`)⌋ / 60⌋, *w* the PC timer's divisor, so a count of 2: every second interrupt, 60 times a second ([the rate](../simulation/timer.md#the-rate)), keeping the slot in `HmiTimer_Slot` (`000b1edc`) |
| `HmiTimer_Remove` | `00050e00` | passes the slot to `Timer_RemoveCallback` (`0006d337`, [callbacks](../simulation/timer.md#callbacks)), which `Ttm_ManagerDtor` gives its own slot |
| `HmiTimer_Enable`, `HmiTimer_Disable` | `00050d80`, `00050d8c` | set `HmiTimer_Enabled` (`000b1ed8`) to 1 and 0 |
| `HmiTimer_Tick` | `00050d38` | while enabled and not already inside the callback (the count `000b1ee8`), calls the callback through the far pointer |
| `HmiTimer_NoOp`, `HmiHook_NoOp` | `00050e10`, `00050e14` | return at once |
| `HmiLock_RegionStart`, `HmiLock_RegionEnd` | `00050e18`, `00050e1c` | a `RET` each: the HMI code passes the four bytes between them as a region to lock and unlock (`X32_UnlockRegion`, `00060b91`) |

`es2_xref.py` finds the calls of all but the tick and the markers in the HMI code at `0006d4f5`–`0006da46`, and none to `HmiTimer_TickUnguarded` (`00050d60`) or its copy `HmiTimer_TickUnguarded2` (`00050d70`), which call the callback while enabled with no count. The file ends with the second marker and three zero bytes.

## Loading the banks

`SoundManager_LoadBanks` (`0004f6d4`, `ES1GO 00042bf9`) reads `sos\melodic.bnk`, `sos\drum.bnk` and `sos\drum32.dig`, in that order, through the manager's volume stream `+0x504`. For each it opens the file with `VolRStream_Open`, allocates the size `VolRStream_GetSize` gives, reads that many bytes with the stream's read slot `+0x1c`, closes the file and keeps a far pointer to the data at `+0x13a`, `+0x146` and `+0x140`. It returns 0 at the first that fails to open, allocate or read, and 1 after the third. [The driver](#the-driver) says where each goes; the files are [the banks](../formats/sound-samples.md#the-banks).

## Open

- **Open:** what the `0x13` `HmiDigi_InitDriver` passes to `00080a0a` is. Whether the samples play at the rate it passes as the image's call 2 is [the mixer's](digital-mixer.md#open) item.
- **Open:** the HMI calls `SoundManager_DetectDigitalCard`'s helpers make (`0007235d`, `00072573`, `00072901`, `00072ce9`, `00072517`) and the buffer's fields they fill, for module E.
- **Open:** what a `resource.cfg` with a `[SoundFX]` `Driver#` and a blank `[Music]` `Driver#` plays, since the MIDI device's open comes first in `SoundManager_LoadDrivers` ([the driver](#the-driver)) and a blank `Driver#` passes what the stack held ([the mission start's Open](../simulation/main-loop.md#open)). A retail check: set the music to none in the setup and play a mission; the click and the footfalls are heard or not.
