# GO.EXE — repairing, building and scrapping HERCs

**After a career mission the service bay's REPAIR and BUILD open small windows down the right of the hangar's picture. REPAIR shows the selected HERC's type, pilot, condition and the salvage a full repair needs; REPAIR ALL repairs it as far as the salvage reaches, REPAIR opens a second window that repairs one system at a time, a level at a time, with + and −, SCRAP turns the HERC into salvage, and PREFS sets which HERCs the shell repairs on its own after a mission. BUILD shows one chassis at a time with its mass, speed and capacity and its price; BUILD pays for it at once and the HERC takes one to three missions to finish, during which the same button reads SCRAP and gives the salvage back.** The bay's bar is [between missions](between-missions.md#the-service-bays-bar)'s, the bay's own display and CREW [hangar.md](hangar.md)'s, and fitting weapons [arming.md](arming.md)'s. ES2's repair, build and scrap screens are VSHELL's ([the repair screen](../../retail/shell/weapons-and-repair.md#the-repair-screen), [the build screen](../../retail/shell/build-and-armory.md#the-build-screen)); the twins are named in [Functions](#functions).

Addresses are `ES1GO` image addresses ([the X-32 executable](../formats/x32-executable.md#the-image)). The functions here remove their own arguments, so argument lists are in stack order ([calling conventions](../formats/compiler.md#calling-conventions)). A screen's lists are kind-15 list boxes of style 1 used as readouts: the handlers set a list's lines with its slot `+0x44` and select a line with its slot `+0x24`, and read the selected line with slot `+0x2c` ([the list box](menu-controls.md#the-list-box)). A control is made inactive or active by writing state 2 or 0 to its `+0x19` and 1 to *changed* `+0x1f` ([opening a menu](menus.md#opening-a-menu--menu_open-0003969e)). The selected HERC is the HERC in the hangar's slot `Hangar_SelectedSlot` (`00080894`) of `Hangar_Slots` (`000808a0`) ([the HERC lists](../formats/car-hercs-and-stock.md#the-herc-lists)); "no HERC" below means that slot is −1 or empty.

## Where the object files start and end

**`mechman.cpp`** runs from `0002845c` to `0002a3d7`. The file before, `inventor.cpp` (`arming.md`), ends with a `RET 0xc` at `00028456` and three zero bytes; `Hangar_SaveFile` (`0002909c`) asserts with the path `src\mechman.cpp` (`00080920`, pushed at `000290ce`); and the file ends with a `RET 4` at `0002a3d3` and two zero bytes before the memory log's file at `0002a3d8` ([the memory log's file](main-menu.md#where-the-object-files-start-and-end)). It has no static initializer. `es1_boilerplate.py scan` over it finds one library copy, `Rect_CopyCtorMechManCopy` (`00028a9b`), byte for byte `Rect_CopyCtor` (`0004b398`). It holds the hangar's HERC list ([the HERC lists](../formats/car-hercs-and-stock.md#the-herc-lists)), the selection helpers below, BUILD's information panel, the build, the scrap and the repairs' entry points.

**The screens' code**, `0002ecc0` to `00031367`, is the last part of [the menu handlers' file](main-menu.md#where-the-object-files-start-and-end): REPAIR's, SYSTEM REPAIR's, PREFERENCES', SCRAP's, BUILD's and SALVAGE's handlers and helpers, then the file's static initializer `MenuHandlers_StaticInit` (`00030ee9`, entry `0009a260`) and its exit routine `MenuHandlers_StaticExit` (`00031367`, exit entry `0009a29c`), a bare `PUSH EBP`, `MOV EBP,ESP`, `POP EBP`, `RET`. The initializer stores 18 (*x*, *y*) dword pairs at `000a04dc`: (37, 72), (29, 69), (23, 72), (19, 66), (21, 63), (0, 0), (34, 72), (26, 72), (18, 70), (36, 67), (32, 66), (32, 60), (17, 71), (42, 71), (28, 75), (17, 74), (25, 74) and (29, 69); `es2_xref.py --decode` finds them read by ARMING's hotspot handlers `Arming_DrawWeaponInfo` (`0002d5bf`) and `Arming_DrawMissileInfo` (`0002dacf`) (`arming.md`). `es1_boilerplate.py scan` over the range finds `HercStatus_GetMenuHandlersCopy` (`000304c6`), byte for byte `HercStatus_Get` (`00015896`), and `Tech_IsAvailableMenuHandlersCopy` (`00030e83`), byte for byte `Tech_IsAvailable` (`00028409`).

## The hangar's selection

**The hangar's screens work on one HERC at a time, the selected one, and step through the HERCs with the HERCS arrows; the HERC being built is skipped.** `mechman.cpp`'s helpers do the stepping and the screen change that goes with it.

- `Hangar_NextHercSlot` (`00029729`)() adds 1 to `Hangar_SelectedSlot` modulo the slot count `Hangar_SlotCount` (`00080890`) until the slot holds a HERC and is not the marked slot `Hangar_MarkedSlot` (`00080896`), or comes back to where it started; `Hangar_PrevHercSlot` (`000296c9`)() does the same downwards, from slot 0 to the last. The modulo divides by 0 in an empty hangar, and the test for the start comes after the step, so from slot −1 with no slot to stop at the step never ends.
- `Hangar_NextUnpilotedHercSlot` (`0002978c`)() and `Hangar_PrevUnpilotedHercSlot` (`000297cb`)() step with those until `CareerRecord_FindPilotBySlot` (`00015c39`)(`CareerRecord_Current` (`00014ef4`), slot) answers −1, a HERC no pilot holds, or the selection comes back to where it started.
- `Hangar_SelectMarkedSlot` (`0002980a`)() gives the marked slot `Hangar_FindFreeSlot` (`0002983a`)'s answer when it is −1, and selects it.
- `Hangar_SelectNext` (`0002986f`)(mode) calls `Hangar_LeaveSelectedHerc` (`000295e7`), then by mode: 0 `Hangar_NextHercSlot`; 1 `CareerRecord_NextPilot` (`00015b0a`)(the roster's current record, `0009b23b` + `0x1f7` × the word `0009b21f`, read directly rather than through `CareerRecord_Current`); 2 `Hangar_NextUnpilotedHercSlot`; 3 `Hangar_SelectMarkedSlot` and, when the marked slot is then not −1, `HangarBay_AssignSlotScripts` (`000120f9`)(the marked slot, 1). It then wipes the screen to color 0 with `Screen_BlindsClear` (`0004c053`)(8, 0, 1), shows the new HERC with `Hangar_ShowSelectedHerc` (`000294d8`), selects no mount with `Hangar_SelectMount` (`00029a14`)(−1), and wipes the new picture in with `Screen_BlindsCopy` (`0004bc0d`)(8, the display's visible page `+0x30`, its draw page `+0x38`, 1).
- `Hangar_SelectPrev` (`00029946`)(mode) is the same downwards for modes 0 to 2 (`Hangar_PrevHercSlot`, `CareerRecord_PrevPilot` (`00015b85`), `Hangar_PrevUnpilotedHercSlot`), with no mode 3, and wipes and shows only when the selected slot is then not −1.

`Hangar_LeaveSelectedHerc` (`000295e7`)() calls `Herc_EraseFromBay` (`000241cd`)(the selected HERC) and `Hangar_ShowSelectedHerc` (`000294d8`)(), for a selected slot that is not −1, sets the pages of the bay's group scripts by the slot ([the group's scripts](menus.md#the-backdrop)) with `Ttm_SetPage`, on the handles of the 3-byte entries 0, 1 and 2 of `ShellBackdrop_GroupTtms`: entry 0's script to the page `000aa7b0`[slot] + 1, entry 1's to `000aa7c0`[slot] + 1 and entry 2's to slot mod 8 + 1; then, for a HERC, `Herc_DrawInBay` (`000241f9`)(HERC, the marked slot's count `Hangar_MarkedMissionsLeft` (`00080898`) for the marked slot, otherwise 0), and last `ShellBackdrop_RunScripts` (`00012467`)() ([showing a bay](hangar.md#showing-a-bay)). `HangarBay_AssignSlotScripts` (`000120f9`)(slot, mode) gives a slot its scripts and picks those two words, by mode ([giving the scripts their bays](hangar.md#giving-the-scripts-their-bays)): 0, `TtmEntry_AssignSlot` (`00012047`)(slot, 6), a draw below 5 into `000aa7b0`[slot] and 2 into `000aa7c0`[slot]; 2, `TtmEntry_AssignSlot`(slot, slot + 9), into `000aa7b0`[slot] 3 plus a draw below 2 for slot 0 and a draw below 5 for any other, and into `000aa7c0`[slot] a draw below 5 when the chassis of the slot's HERC (−1 for none) is below 5 and below 3 otherwise; 1, `TtmEntry_FreeSlot` (`000120ba`)(slot), then, with no marked HERC or a selected HERC at 0% built (`Hangar_SelectedBuildProgress`, `0002a2a1`), `TtmEntry_AssignSlot`(slot, 8) and 3 plus a draw below 2 into `000aa7b0`[slot], and otherwise `TtmEntry_AssignSlot`(slot, 7), `TtmEntry_AssignSlot`(slot, the entry the word `0007e700`[the selected HERC's chassis]) while the selected HERC is below 51% built, and a draw below 3 into `000aa7b0`[slot]; for mode 1, a draw below 5 into `000aa7c0`[slot]. The draws are `Math_RandomBelow`(`Shell_RandomState`, *n*). `Hangar_SelectedBuildProgress` (`0002a2a1`)() is `Hangar_MarkedHercProgress` (`00029f93`)'s (*c* − `Hangar_MarkedMissionsLeft`) × 100 / *c* on the selected HERC's chassis ([the marked HERC](between-missions.md#the-marked-herc)), with chassis −1 for no HERC, which reads the word before `Chassis_BuildMissions` (`000808d8`), `000808d6`, 46 in the image, as *c*.

**The wipes.** `Screen_BlindsCopy` (`0004bc0d`)(*n*, *to*, *from*, flag) installs the full-screen render context `0007fcd8`, sets the shell display `000ab616`'s source page `+0x34` to *from* and its draw page `+0x38` to *to* (through the raster's routines `000865f0` and `000865f4` while it is the current display), and in *n* passes copies bands 320 wide from *from* to *to* with `Region_CopyBetweenPages`, pass *i* the rects from (0, *r*) to (`0x13f`, *r* + 1) for *r* = *i*, *i* + *n*, *i* + 2*n*, … below the word `0009b00c`, the bottom of the rect `0009b000`; the raster's rects are inclusive, so each band is two rows and overlaps the next pass's, waiting a tick (`Shell_WaitTicks`(1)) after each pass; then it puts both pages and the render context back. Its fourth argument is not read. `Screen_BlindsClear` (`0004c053`)(*n*, color, flag) clears the draw page to the color through the raster's routine `0008663c` ([lines and clipped bitmaps](../rendering/screen-pages.md#lines-and-clipped-bitmaps)), blinds it over the visible page with `Screen_BlindsCopy`(*n*, the visible page `+0x30`, the draw page, flag), and copies the region `Shell_FullScreenRegion` (`0009aff8`) from page 2, the background (`0007fccc`), onto the draw page. So the HERCS arrows black the screen out in eight interleaved passes, a tick apart, and bring the new HERC in the same way.

**Mounts.** `Hangar_SelectMount` (`00029a14`)(mount), `Hangar_NextMount` (`00029a52`)() and `Hangar_PrevMount` (`00029a87`)() call, on the selected HERC, `Herc_HighlightMount` (`00024326`)(HERC, mount), which makes *mount* the current mount `+0x70`, `Herc_HighlightNextMount` (`000242a6`)(HERC), which steps it forward modulo the mount count, and `Herc_HighlightPreviousMount` (`000242e8`)(HERC), which steps it back; `Hangar_ClearSelectedMount` (`0002961c`)() calls `Herc_HideMountHighlight` (`00024287`)(HERC), which erases the mounts, turns the outline off and draws the mounts again, leaving the current mount `+0x70` as it was ([the mount highlight](../formats/herc-diagrams.md#the-mount-highlight)). `Hangar_FitWeapon` (`00029abc`)(weapon) fits a weapon to the selected HERC's current mount: nothing when the mount already holds *weapon*; the condition is 100 for weapon 17, the empty mount, and otherwise the word `Inventory_Take` (`0002833f`)(the stock `000aad62` points to, weapon) pops, nothing more happening when that is −1; then `Herc_EraseMounts` (`00024561`)(HERC), and, when the mount held a weapon other than 17, `Inventory_Add` (`00028311`) of the mount's condition (`Herc_CurrentMountStatus` (`00024b51`)(HERC)) onto the old weapon's slot, so the old weapon goes back to the stock; then `Herc_SetCurrentMountStatus` (`00024aea`)(HERC, condition), `Herc_SetCurrentMountWeapon` (`00024788`)(HERC, weapon) and `Herc_DrawMounts` (`00024670`)(HERC). ARMING calls these (`arming.md`). `Hangar_ReloadSelectedLoadout` (`00028ec9`)(), outside `Shell_Mode` 1, opens entry 10 of the file-name table, `car\sm_play.mec` ([the squad](../formats/msn-mission-file.md#the-squad)), as a `FileRStream` and runs `HercList_RefitSelectedFromStream` (`00028f16`)(stream), which passes over the list's 12 header bytes and, for each HERC, reads its slot; the HERC in the selected slot is read into a HERC of its own, and for each of its mounts `Hangar_SelectMount`(mount) and `Hangar_FitWeapon`(that HERC's weapon on the mount) refit the selected HERC; the others are passed over field by field. `es2_xref.py` finds no caller of `Hangar_ReloadSelectedLoadout` ([Open](#open)).

### REPAIR ALL

**REPAIR ALL repairs the selected HERC as far down the after-mission repair ladder as the whole salvage pool reaches.** Its case calls `Repair_AutoSelected` (`00029bdd`)(), which is `Repair_Auto` (`00029bf3`)(`Career_Salvage`, `Hangar_SelectedSlot`) ([repairs](between-missions.md#repairs)): from level 0, the full repair to 100, one level down while the cost is above the pool; when the cost is then below the pool it sets every status word to the level's target, takes the cost from `Career_Salvage`, and stores pool − cost as the share `+0x21` of the pilot entry that holds the slot, the record's dword `+0x10` when none does. Then `Hangar_ShowSlot` (`00029651`)(slot) shows the bay again ([showing a bay](hangar.md#showing-a-bay)), 1905 selects the new level, `Repair_LevelForCondition` of the overall condition (0 for no HERC), through its slot `+0x24`, `RepairMenu_RefreshSalvage` runs and `Roster_ForEachPilotNoOp` (`00016fdd`)(the roster) does nothing. ## Open

- **Open:** a retail check under DOSBox-X of PROCEED after +: in a career after the first mission, REPAIR, then REPAIR on a HERC with damage; select an internal part whose STATUS reads MODERATE DAMAGE and press + once (STATUS reads LIGHT DAMAGE, ALLOCATED shows the cost); PROCEED, then REPAIR again: the code sets the part to NOMINAL's floor, 91, so STATUS reads NOMINAL.
- **Open:** whether SYSTEM REPAIR's weapon list prices a mount by its number rather than its weapon in retail: `Repair_UnitValueCost` reads `000ab332`[item], and which weapon each of the 18 values prices is open ([the shell's value tables](../formats/herc-catalogs.md#open)).
- **Open:** what opens screen 2010, SALVAGE: a byte search of `ES1GO` for the value 2010 found it only in `Shell_ScreenIds` (`0008181a`) and the case table (`00082100`), and `HercWorks.Query menu` lists no control with the id 2010.
- **Open:** no caller of `Hangar_ReloadSelectedLoadout` (`00028ec9`) found by `es2_xref.py`.
- **Open:** what the kind-16 controls of PREFERENCES draw for their word 1 and 0 (`MTGfxListBox_Ctor` (`0001eaef`), [the kinds](../formats/dlg-menu-dialogs.md#open)): the frames of `res32\check.dba`, whose count and pictures are unread, and whether `000813d4` is always 0 when PREFERENCES opens, or still holds another menu's bank, which the tiles would then draw.
- **Open:** whether anything frees the readouts' arrays: `es2_xref.py --decode` finds `00080e14`, `00080e18` and `00080e1c` only stored (and `00080e1c` read back by `RepairMenu_RefreshSalvage` right after its own store), and the two screens' destructors do not free them.
- **Open:** a retail check of BUILD's bay picture: open BUILD from the bay after a mission with a free bay and enough salvage, and BUILD; whether the bay behind the window shows the new HERC before DONE, which `FabricationMenu_OnMessage`'s build branch does not draw.
- **Open:** a retail check of BUILD: open BUILD from the bay after a mission; the panel reads TYPE: ROADRUNNER, MASS: 18 TONS, SPEED: 84 KPH, CAPACITY: 3 WEAPONS, SALVAGE REQUIRED: 50 TONS; BUILD, then the panel reads UNDER CONSTRUCTION, % COMPLETE: 0 and SALVAGE ALLOCATED: 50 TONS, and the button reads SCRAP.
