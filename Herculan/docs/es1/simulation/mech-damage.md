# DBSIM.EXE — the HERC's hits and damage: the hit test, blasts and the damage write

**ES1's HERC takes hits through ES2's hit test, blast and damage write, on ES1's fields, the blast and the damage write called directly where ES2's go through the HERC's table ([the HERC's table](mech.md#the-hercs-table)).** What a player would see differently:

- a blast, a shot's splash and a collision with another HERC reach its components without passing its shields, and its own code never resizes its shield array as the generator is shot;
- only the two cockpits or the pilot kill it, not life support; the leg grades are coarser; and of its own damage this file's code posts only the message for a shot getting through the player's shields, where ES2's file also posts the leg, weapon and reactor messages (whether other ES1 code posts them is [Open](#open));
- when the player's HERC is disabled, its body turns to face where the turret pointed and the turret centers;
- every hit that lands throws debris, where ES2's throws it only when the hit changes the component's damage band.

Addresses are `ES1DBSIM` image addresses ([the X-32 executable](../formats/x32-executable.md#the-image)); ES2's are DBSIM's. Every function here removes its own arguments, so argument lists are in stack order, the machine first and the other arguments in the reverse of ES2's order ([calling conventions](../formats/compiler.md#calling-conventions)). The HERC's fields are [the HERC's](mech.md#the-fields).

## The hit test — `Mech_DirectFireHitTest` (`00013b44`)

Slot `+0x24`, (machine, shot): ES2's hit test and `Mech_ApplyDirectFireDamage` in one function ([direct-fire damage](../../retail/simulation/damage-system.md#direct-fire-damage-armor-then-part-deterministic-shield-gated)), on ES1's fields.

1. The coarse reject as ES2's; then the copies of the shot's `+6` (the shields' figure) and `+4` (the armor's), both scaled by [`Damage_ScaleByDifficulty`](world.md#the-difficulty-scale--damage_scalebydifficulty-0001f82c) when the owner's group's side byte is 0.
2. A machine other than the player's turns its radar on for a shot of type 0 or 1 unless the radar silence counter `+0x201` runs, and off with the counter at 6000 for type 2, as ES2's.
3. `Mech_ShieldAbsorb_DirectFire` (`0000fca8`, [the shields](shields-and-behaviour-states.md#the-shields)) on the shot in the machine's frame. With no entry it returns 0.
4. With one: `+0x8e` cleared and `+0x21e` raised by what the shields took. A shot the shields stopped makes its impact at the entry point. Otherwise the player's machine posts computer message 8 once, latched on `+0xaa`, where ES2's posts `SHIELDS CRITICAL` while front and rear together hold under 500; then `Mech_SelectStruckComponent` (`00009064`)(model, flags, frame, shot, machine) picks the component ([the struck component](component-damage.md#the-struck-component--mech_selectstruckcomponent-00009064)), and a miss returns 0.
5. On a struck component, ES2's damage application ([the chance path](../../retail/simulation/weapon-mounts.md#the-chance-path--the-destruction-roll)): `+0x21e` raised by the armor figure, the splash Q10(armor, shot `+8`), the reading before from `Component_ReadDamagePercent` (`0000a16c`), `Mech_ComponentDamageWrite`(machine, owner, armor − splash, component), and with a splash `Mech_ApplyExplosiveDamage`(machine, owner, 500, point, splash). When the band (`>> 5`) changed, a hit on component 0 or 1 of the player's machine starts `Cockpit_StartHitShake` (`0002e17c`), where ES2's also wants the reading under 100 and posts message `0x12`, and the mount knock-out roll runs as ES2's, its salvage at Q10(500, reading) for side 1 and its cascade through `Component_ApplyDamageAndCascade` (`00009f7c`)(header, 10000, component).
6. When the answer below is not 0 and a struck component does not now read `0x100`: the impact effect, `Explosion_Construct` (`00005af0`) with the shot's effect table `+0xa` at 4 × *n* + `Math_RandomNext` & 3, *n* 0 for a stopped shot, 1 for an unchanged band and 2 for a changed one; and then, inside the same test and only for a struck component, `Debris_ThrowGroup` (`00005fb8`, [the debris](../formats/debris-dat.md)) with group *n* of `g_DebrisGroups` (`000bc1c0`), 1 or 2, the point and the roots `g_DebrisShapes` (`000bc1c6`). So a struck component whose distance is 0, or that the hit leaves reading `0x100`, makes no effect and throws nothing. ES2's throws debris only for band 2, and makes the impact even when the component is gone.

It returns the entry distance, the component's after a strike.

## A blast — `Mech_ApplyExplosiveDamage` (`00013a7c`)

(machine, attacker, radius, point, damage), called by `Damage_ExplosiveBlastSweep` (`0001f75c`, [blasts](world.md#blasts--damage_explosiveblastsweep-0001f75c)), the hit test's splash and the collision: ES2's per-component roll and falloff ([a mech](../../retail/simulation/damage-system.md#a-mech--mech_applyexplosivedamage-004187d0)) with no facing and no shields. Each of the 28 components whose occupancy flag is set and whose roll `Math_RandomNext` & `0xfff` falls under `0x802` is placed with slot `+0x5c` and, inside the radius, takes damage × (radius − distance) / radius through `Mech_ComponentDamageWrite`.

## The damage write — `Mech_ComponentDamageWrite` (`00013250`)

(machine, attacker, damage, component), the endpoint ([component damage](../../retail/simulation/component-damage.md#the-component-damage-system)), called directly by every writer here.

1. **Refusals.** On the player's machine, nothing while `MissionModeFlag` is 0 and script.dat header word 6 (`000bc4a8`) is 1, where ES2's asks `Sim_DamageToPlayerDisabled`; and nothing for a component whose occupancy flag is clear.
2. **The cascade**, `Component_ApplyDamageAndCascade` (`00009f7c`)(header, damage, component) ([write and cascade](component-damage.md#write-and-cascade--component_applydamageandcascade-00009f7c)), with no mount snapshot before it.
3. **Mounts.** Every mount that is present, not yet destroyed (`+0x49`) and whose component's occupancy flag reads clear is destroyed with `WeaponMount_Destroy` (`0000b968`)(mount, 0, machine), whichever component this write struck: the component is 19 plus the signed byte `+0x17` of the mount's hardpoint record (its `.GL` record at `+0x18`), and the mounts are the manager's array, its count the `int16` at the manager's `+4`. ES2's calls each mount's condition slot with the readings before and after, and posts `WEAPON DESTROYED` ([losing a mount](weapon-mount-classes.md#losing-a-mount)).
4. **No shield recompute.** ES2's runs `Mech_ComputeShieldCapacity` here.
5. **Legs**, for a record whose leg count `+0x4a` is not 0, for any chassis. Leg *i*, 0 to 3, reads internal 0, 1, 10 and 11 in turn ([the readings](component-damage.md#the-readings)), legs 2 and 3 only when the count is above 2, and a negative damage word reads `0x100`. Each leg below the count that reads `0x100` destroys its shadow, slot *i* of `+0x1cc` when it is not null, through the shadow's slot `+0xc` with 3, and nulls the slot; the count `+0x1d0` is left as it was ([shadows and footfalls](mech-locomotion.md#shadows-and-footfalls--mech_placelegsonground-000144dc)). ES2's queues the shadow. Then, when the machine is not immobilized (`+0x9e`), the legs reading `0x100` are counted in order, and the machine is immobilized when the count reaches the leg count `>> 1`: while it is not destroyed (`+0x93`), the attacker's slot `+0x64`(attacker, 0, machine) when there is an attacker, `Mech_ReportOutOfAction` (`0000d77c`), the action `+0x170` and `disabled`; then `+0x9e` is set, destroyed or not. Only when it is not immobilized are the legs graded: the two sides are legs 0 and 1, each averaged with leg 2 or 3 (the sum `>> 1`) when the count is 4, and either side at `0xc1` or more sets `+0xa3`, otherwise either above `0x80` sets `+0xa2`, both signed comparisons; neither is cleared here. ES2's grades at `0x8d` and `0x50`, releases the target and posts the leg messages.
6. **Disabled by the player.** In the immobilizing branch, with an attacker that is `LocalPlayerMech` (`000b5b86`) and whose target `+0x15e` is the machine, computer message 7, ES2's `0x2f`.
7. **The player's machine disabled.** When the machine is `LocalPlayerMech`, the same branch turns the body to where the turret pointed: `Rotation_Concat` (`00064fe4`)(the aim node's transform, `Context_GetTransform`(machine + 6), out), the node being the record's `+0xc` part's as `Mech_GetAimNodeTransform` finds it ([small functions](mech.md#small-functions)), a null pointer when the shape has no such part; `Transform_RotationToEuler` (`0005e988`) of the result, whose Z, the third `int16`, becomes the heading `+0xa`. It clears `+0x2c` and the twist `+0x22e`, seeks the twist thread `+0x1c4`, when there is one, with `AnimThread_SeekToPosition` (`000620c4`)(thread, 0, the record's `+0x1c`), and runs `SimObject_ApplyRootMotionIfEnabled` (`0000215c`)(machine, 1). ES2's has no such step.
8. **Death**, while `+0x93` is clear: when `Component_IsFullyDestroyed` (`00009f30`) reports component 0 or 1 fully destroyed, or internal 9, the pilot, reads `0x100`. ES2's also kills on internal 8, life support. In order: `+0x93` set; the attacker, when there is one, told through its slot `+0x64`(attacker, `+0x9e`, machine) with `+0x9e` as it stands after step 5, sign-extended; while that `+0x9e` is clear, the report and the action `+0x170`; the radar `+0x90` off; message 3 (ES2's `0x2e`) when the attacker is `LocalPlayerMech` and that machine's target `+0x15e` is the machine, with no test for a null attacker; then the state. That is `dead` (`000af53c`), or for a record whose `+0x4c` is set: `+0x32` set, the dword Z `+0x28` set to −100000, `in limbo` (`000af514`), and every shadow destroyed and its slot nulled, with the count `+0x1d0` zeroed. ES2's also writes 30000 to component 0 first, releases the target and skips the state for a flyer.
9. **The reactor**, while `+0xa4` and `+0xa5` are clear: internal 5's reading latches `+0xa5` past `0xc0` and `+0xa4` past `0x80`, as ES2's, with no message.

ES2's ends by flushing the shadows' delete queue.

## What the attacker is told — `Mech_CreditNeutralisedTarget` (`00010f2c`)

Slot `+0x64`, (attacker, already immobilized, victim): ES2's ([what the attacker is told](../../retail/simulation/component-damage.md#what-the-attacker-is-told--mech_creditneutralisedtarget-00415710)) with these differences.

- **The kill half**, with the flag 0. Whenever the victim's side byte (its group `+0x3f`'s `+0x14`) is not the attacker's, the attacker's tally, the `int16` at `+0x23a` + 2 × the victim's target class `+0x162` ([the object fields](mission-groups.md#the-object-fields)), goes up one, as ES2's does. The call-outs need the attacker in `PlayerMech`'s (`000bc42a`) group as well, the same group pointer `+0x3f`: for `PlayerMech` itself, the nearest live squadmate, `Group_NearestLiveMember` (`0001cea4`, [the group's target](mission-groups.md#the-groups-target)), calls out 5 with `Ai_PostSquadMessage` (`0001a188`)(squadmate, 0, 5) when `Math_DistanceBetweenPoints` puts it under 30000; any other attacker calls out 2 itself, (attacker, 0, 2). It sets no latch.
- **The victim's cry**, whatever the flag: a victim in `PlayerMech`'s group that is not `PlayerMech` itself posts (victim, 1, `0x25`) when destroyed (`+0x93`) and (victim, 1, 4) otherwise, the 1 forcing the post past a destroyed machine, as ES2's does. ES2's also counts the player's kills.

## Open

- **Open:** whether anything sets the shield capacity after `Shield_Init`: `es2_fieldscan.py` over `00001000`–`0005b000` finds no write of a machine's `+0x1be` (positive control: `+0x228`, written by `Mech_Constructor` and `Mech_PerTickSystemsUpdate`), and the shields' file writes the block's `+6` only in `Shield_Init`; a write through another copy of the block's address is outside that search. A retail check: the shield rings' full extent after the shield generator is shot compared with before.
- **Open:** whether code outside this file posts a message for a leg, a weapon or the reactor damaged, which ES2's damage write posts and ES1's does not. A retail check: lose a weapon to fire and watch the message line.
- **Open:** what computer messages 3, 7 and 8 say in ES1's `str\system.str` ([the string files](cockpit-messages.md#the-string-files)).
- **Open:** what header word 6 (`000bc4a8`), which with the mode `MissionModeFlag` (`000bc4a0`) at 0 refuses the player's damage, means ([the header](../formats/script-dat.md#the-header)).
