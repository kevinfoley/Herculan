# DBSIM.EXE — the HERC's systems tick: energy, countdowns, ECM and missile lock

**Once a tick each HERC in the fight recharges its energy pool and shares it between its weapons and its shields, runs its countdowns, jams or is jammed, and builds missile lock, as ES2's mech does, on ES1's fields.** What a player would see differently:

- its reactor recharges the energy pool at a rate of 15 where ES2's has 20, and reactor damage cuts that rate from the next tick on; the pool holds 1500 back from the weapons where ES2's holds 500, so the energy meter stops falling at 15% however hard the weapons draw ([the energy](#the-systems-tick--mech_perticksystemsupdate-00014ee0)).

Addresses are `ES1DBSIM` image addresses ([the X-32 executable](../formats/x32-executable.md#the-image)); ES2's are DBSIM's. Every function here removes its own arguments, so argument lists are in stack order, the machine first and the other arguments in the reverse of ES2's order ([calling conventions](../formats/compiler.md#calling-conventions)). The HERC's fields are [the HERC's](mech.md#the-fields).

## The systems tick — `Mech_PerTickSystemsUpdate` (`00014ee0`)

(machine), once a tick from `Sim_MainTick` (`00059700`, [the tick](main-loop.md#the-tick--sim_maintick-00059700)) for each machine whose group has no action and that is not destroyed, unless the simulation is frozen: ES2's ([the per-tick cycle](../../retail/simulation/reactor-energy-pool.md#the-per-tick-cycle--mech_perticksystemsupdate-0041aa5c), [missile lock](../../retail/simulation/missile-lock.md)) with these differences.

- **The energy.** The pool `+0x228` gains `Math_IntegrateRateOverTick`(15), scaled by `Math_Q10Multiply` 200 while `+0xa5` is set or 600 while `+0xa4` is. ES2's adds the rate `Mech_ComputeReactorRate` fixed at spawn, 20 or with an Energy Pod 40. The weapons get the budget, the pool less 1500 as an `int16`, through the manager's energy slot (its table at `+0x10`, slot `+4`), (manager, budget) ([energy](weapon-mounts.md#energy--weaponmounts_arbitrateenergy-0000cc2c)); the shields the rest through `Shield_RechargeTick` (`0000fb2c`, [the shields](shields-and-behaviour-states.md#the-shields)); and the pool becomes what is left plus 1500, clamped to [0, 10000]. ES2's holds back 500.
- **The pool stays at 1500 or more.** `Mech_Constructor` sets it to 10000, and each mount's pool turn returns at least 0 from a budget of 0 or more: `WeaponMount_RefireTick`, the turn of the base, ammunition, grenade and ECM classes, returns it whole, and the energy classes' `WeaponMount_ChargeCapacitor`, the ELF's through `ElfMount_SpinUpAndChargeTick`, draws at most the budget and gives up to 5 back ([charging](weapon-mount-classes.md#charging)). So the shields' request is never negative, their grant is 0 to 4, and what is left is never negative. The energy meter, (pool << 10) / 10000 ([the cockpit](mech.md#the-cockpit)), bottoms out at 153 of its `0x400`, 15%. `es2_fieldscan.py` finds no other word write of `+0x228` ([Open](#open)).
- **The shields message** re-arms on the player's machine when the front and the rear each hold more than 300, where ES2's wants more than 1500 together.
- **The countdowns** are ES2's on ES1's fields ([the fields](mech.md#the-fields)): `Timer_CountDown` (`00064178`) on the retarget, complaint and under-fire countdowns, `+0x208`, `+0x20d` and `+0x212`, and `Math_CountdownTimerTick` on the radar silence's, `+0x200`; the under-fire window's expiry zeroes the dword `+0x217`, and the damage window `+0x21b`, at 0, has its counter reset to 4000 and `+0x21e` zeroed. The unstick countdown `+0x203` is not ticked here. With `+0x8e` clear, `Mech_ComputeCombatRating` (`000189f0`, [the combat reassess](mech-ai.md#the-combat-reassess--mech_aicombatreassess-00016e04)).
- **The ECM switch.** With a target `+0x15e` and `Mech_GetEcmPodSwitch` answering non-zero, the machine's ECM mount switched on ([the ECM](weapon-mount-classes.md#the-ecm)), the target's `+0x9c` is set: its gate on its engagement action ([the fields](sensor-model.md#the-fields)), which `Sim_DetectionTick` (`0000e240`) clears on every object in its third pass, just before `Sim_MainTick` runs the systems ticks ([the tick](main-loop.md#the-tick--sim_maintick-00059700), step 6). So the gate stands at every test of the next tick's sweep, and a target the machine jams has its engagement action held back for as long as the switch stays on, as ES2's `+0xa2` does ([an object's own two actions](../../retail/simulation/mission-deployment.md#an-objects-own-two-actions--0x1b2-and-0x1b6)). Then `+0x9b`, jamming, is set to whether `Mech_IsEcmSwitchedOn` answers non-zero.
- **The ECM roll**, ES2's ([ECM](../../retail/simulation/missile-lock.md#ecm)), with a target: when its class `+0x162` is 0, a HERC, and its `+0x9b` is set, `Math_CountdownTimerTick` runs the roll's countdown `+0x1fd`, and when that reads 0, `Math_RandomNext` of the simulation generator `000b5bd4` decides: its low 12 bits below `0x334` (20 × 41) set the spoof `+0x96`, set the counter `+0x1fe` to 5000 and mark the tick spoofed; otherwise `+0x96` is cleared and the counter set to 1500. A target that is not a HERC, or does not jam, clears `+0x96` and leaves the countdown as it stands. ES2's weighs the roll 5 instead of 20 when the holder's Targeting Pod is damaged; ES1 has no Targeting Pod.
- **Lock**, ES2's ([the mechanism](../../retail/simulation/missile-lock.md#the-mechanism)) on ES1's fields, with no line-of-sight test and no turretless branch. Every tick, target or not, `WeaponMounts_RoundsByMissileType` (`0000be6c`) counts the rounds per subtype and the five flags at the manager's `+6` + 2 × subtype and the lamp `+0x95` are cleared. With a target, the reload is `Math_DistanceBetweenPoints`(target `+0x20`, machine `+0x20`) shifted right 2, arithmetic, and held to `0x7fff`, and the bearing is `Math_HeadingToward` from the machine's `+0x20` to the target's, less the heading `+0xa` and plus the twist `+0x22e`, as a signed word. With `+0x97` set or the bearing outside [−`0x3000`, `0x3000`), the countdowns of subtypes 0, 1 and 2 are reloaded, subtype 4's is left alone, and `+0x97` is cleared at the end of the function. Otherwise each subtype with rounds, in the order 0, 4, 1, 2, has its countdown reloaded or run with `Math_CountdownTimerTick`, and a run countdown that reads 0 sets the flag:

| Subtype | Countdown, its counter a byte later | Flag, the manager's | Reloaded while | Sets the flag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `+0x1ee` | `+6` | the machine's radar `+0x90` is off, or the roll spoofed this tick | with `+0x96` clear |
| 4 | `+0x1fa` | `+0xe` | as subtype 0 | with `+0x96` clear |
| 1 | `+0x1f1` | `+8` | the roll spoofed this tick | with `+0x96` clear |
| 2 | `+0x1f4` | `+0xa` | the target's radar `+0x90` and jamming `+0x9b` are both clear | whatever `+0x96` holds |

- **The lamp**, for the player's machine (`+0x9d`) with a target, as ES2's ([the lock lamp](../../retail/simulation/missile-lock.md#the-lock-lamp)): a negative armed index, the manager's signed byte `+0x1d`, counts as subtype 5; otherwise the armed mount's slot `+0x64` answers its subtype, called with (mount, 0), a null out-pointer, which `WeaponMount_GetAmmoType` (`0000ac70`) and `WeaponMount_GetEnergyAmmoType` (`0000ac54`) test before writing through it. Subtype 5 lights `+0x95` when any of the five flags is set, another subtype when its own flag is. Then `Mech_LockTonePlay` runs.

**`Mech_LockTonePlay` (`0001552c`)**, (machine): ES2's ([the lock lamp](../../retail/simulation/missile-lock.md#the-lock-lamp)) on `+0x95` and `+0x97`, its two latches `00091c5d` and `00091c5c`, and the phase from bit `0x40` of now, `000981c4`: with the lamp lit it sets `LockTone_WasLocked` and plays `Sound_Play`(`0x15`), the lock's beep, once each phase the bit is set; with it out, it plays `0x16`, the lock lost, once when `LockTone_WasLocked` is set, clearing it, and otherwise `0x14` on a tick `+0x97`, the target changed, is set.

## Open

- **Open:** whether anything but `Mech_Constructor` and the systems tick writes the energy pool `+0x228`, which would let the pool fall under the 1500 reserve and the shields give charge back to it ([the shields](shields-and-behaviour-states.md#the-shields)): `es2_fieldscan.py --binary ES1DBSIM 228 --writes-only` finds word writes from those two alone, the constructor's 10000 and the tick's three, and dword writes of other structures' `+0x228`. A retail check: hold an energy weapon's trigger until its charge bar stalls and see the energy meter stop at about 15% of its length.
