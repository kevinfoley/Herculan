# Cut and unreachable content

Retail content of *Metaltech: Earthsiege* that was authored, shipped in the data files or the binary, and cannot be reached by playing the game. Two audiences: someone reading the data files who finds a weapon or a record that appears real and is not, and someone interested in what the game was going to have. *Earthsiege 2*'s list is [`retail/cut-content.md`](../retail/cut-content.md).

## Weapons

- **`GREN` — the grenade launcher** (weapon 5, `GRENADE LAUNCHER` in the shell's text), incomplete and unusable. No retail HERC carries it, and neither tech table makes it available: `TECH_0.DAT` (the campaign) and `TECH_SNG.DAT` (single missions) both hold −1 for it, so the armory never offers it ([the retail files](formats/bst-battle-sets.md#the-retail-files)). The launcher's half is complete: its mount class has a hold-to-throw trigger whose throw distance grows from 20000 to 100000 ([triggers](simulation/weapon-mount-classes.md#triggers)), and a magazine of 8 it spends a round of on each throw ([firing](simulation/weapon-mount-classes.md#firing)). The grenade's half is a shell: the throw calls a function that only returns, the grenade class's loader is empty, so nothing fills the tables its constructor reads, and its tick never ends ([the grenade](simulation/projectiles.md#the-grenade)). Its record's projectile index, 22, points past the end of the projectile table, whose grenade records are 7 to 9 ([the projectile index](formats/weapons-dat-sim.md#the-projectile-index)). In the simulator it has no art of its own: its model is the missile racks' shape 5 of `dts\mechwpns` and its damage icon theirs ([retail values](formats/weapons-dat-sim.md#retail-values)). The shell has some. `SHELL0.VOL` holds its ARMAMENT animation, `W1800W05.TTM` with the 16 frames of `W1800W05.DBA`, a wireframe that fills in to a box with two barrels ([the animation](shell/arming.md#the-animation)); its low-memory still, frame 5 of `ALLWEAP.DBA`, is an 8×2 placeholder. No table gives it a picture of its ammunition, ARMAMENT listing types for the missile racks alone. Six chassis' diagram tables place its picture on a mount, all but the Raptor's and the Colossus', with an outline for each; the Samson's three pictures and every damaged picture are missing from their banks ([the mount pictures](formats/herc-diagrams.md#the-mount-pictures)). `SIMVOL0.VOL`'s `GRENADE.BND` is byte for byte *Earthsiege 2*'s ([`.BND` notes](formats/bnd-notes.md)). *Earthsiege 2* keeps the grenade class, its throw a bare return, and serves the Locust Launcher `LAEW` with it, itself cut ([*Earthsiege 2*'s weapons](../retail/cut-content.md#weapons)).

- **`W1800G01` — a second finned round**, an ARMAMENT animation nothing shows. `SHELL0.VOL`'s `RES32\` holds two scripts beside the weapons' `W1800W*` and the missile types' `W1800R0*`: `W1800G00.TTM` and `W1800G01.TTM`, each with its bank, near twins of a finned round with a short thick body, `W1800G01.DBA`'s 8 pixels longer and a frame more. `W1800G00.DBA` is in use, as the ELECTRO-OPTICAL missile's picture, through `W1800R03.TTM` ([the animation](shell/arming.md#the-animation)). A byte search of every entry of the seven archives finds `1800G` in those two scripts and `W1800R03.TTM` only, and `GO.EXE` holds the name formats `w1800w0%d.ttm`, `w1800w%d.ttm` and `w1800r0%d.ttm` and none with a `g`.

## Miscellaneous features

- **Hover tank**. It is classified as a flyer, but very incomplete. There are only two files for this, `HOVTANK.COL` (a collider) and `HOVTANK.DMG` (an armor/health configuration file, in a different format than other DMG files).
