# Known Issues — Metaltech: Earthsiege

## Metaltech: Earthsiege (original retail game)

_Most bugs in this section are read from the disassembly and not yet checked in the running game, which runs under DOSBox-X; an entry says so when it has been. Bugs in this section are reproduced as-is in HERCULAN Engine unless otherwise noted._

_Some of the bugs in this section were filed by Claude and are un-verified. Claude frequently concludes that a memory address/feature is unreachable and unused in retail, when in fact the mechanism for accessing this address/feature simply has not been found yet; any claim to this effect should be viewed with skepticism._

_Note to Claude: Detailed technical descriptions belong in their respective docs, not here. Give a short plain-English summary._

### Cosmetic
- The clouds drift by a fixed step every frame drawn, so their speed depends on the frame rate. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, which moves them 25 times a second. See [`docs/es1/rendering/ground-and-sky.md`](docs/es1/rendering/ground-and-sky.md#each-frame).
- A turret aimed slowly with a joystick moves in visible steps, as in Earthsiege 2: its pose is rounded to a whole animation step. **Fixed** in HERCULAN Engine as a Tweak that is on by default. See [`docs/es1/rendering/dts-node-posing.md`](docs/es1/rendering/dts-node-posing.md#differences-from-es2).
- On a stereo sound card, a sound's left/right placement follows a fixed compass direction rather than the way the HERC faces, so turning the HERC does not move it. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, whose "Stereo sound" Tweak, on by default, pans by the camera's view. See [`docs/es1/formats/sound-samples.md`](docs/es1/sound/sound-manager.md#placed-sounds).
- The flyers' engine sound is played from whichever flyer moved first in the frame, not the nearest one: the check meant to keep the nearest compares a distance with itself. Reproduced as-is; playing it from the nearest flyer is an optional Tweak in HERCULAN Engine. See [`docs/es1/formats/sound-samples.md`](docs/es1/sound/sound-manager.md#placed-sounds).
- With a music device other than FM synthesis (Sound Master II, MPU 401, MT-32 or AWE 32), many songs lose most of their parts or play nothing at all, since they were written for FM. On MT-32 only the drums play. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, which always plays the songs on FM. See [`docs/es1/sound/song-player.md`](docs/es1/sound/song-player.md#the-devices).
- After returning from the outside view, the cockpit's left-most column flickers every frame, shown a row out of place on every other frame: the copy that puts the cockpit back is one column too wide. Seen in the running game. **Fixed** in HERCULAN Engine as the "Fix cockpit edge flicker" Tweak, on by default. See `Es1ViewManager.KeepCockpitEdge` and [`docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-views.md`](docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-views.md#cockpitview_stepviewtransition-00022904).
- After a glance to the left or right, the forward view keeps the side views' plain cursor, which no longer turns into the gunsight's cursor over the gunsight, until the player looks down at the Heads-Down Display and back up: the return from a glance installs the side views' cursor rather than the forward view's. Reported by a player and matched in the disassembly. Reproduced as-is; the "Fix cursor after side view" Tweak, off by default and recommended, gives the forward view its own cursor back. See `Es1ViewManager.ForwardCursorAfterGlance` and [`docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-views.md`](docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-views.md#the-cursor-after-a-glance).
- After changing your own HERC on the CREW screen and pressing CANCEL, your HERC stays painted in the service bay, and the next HERC shown there, with HERCS on the ARMAMENT screen for one, is drawn on top of it. Read from the disassembly and seen in HERCULAN Engine's faithful port; not yet checked in the running game. **Fixed** in HERCULAN Engine as the "Fix HERC Bay art overlap" Tweak, on by default. See `Es1CrewAssignMenu.CancelRestoresFirst` and [`docs/es1/shell/hangar.md`](docs/es1/shell/hangar.md#the-buttons).
- The reactor gauge can draw stray stripes around itself and stop showing the true energy level, most often with little energy left: the hit shake moves the gauge's bars but not the outline that cuts them, so energy used or regained while the cockpit shakes is drawn a few rows off. Reproduced as-is; the "Fix graphical glitches when hit" Tweak, off by default and recommended, moves the outline with the shake. Seen on the TOMAHAWK in HERCULAN Engine's faithful port. See `Es1Cockpit.DrawsFollowShake` and [`docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-energy-shields.md`](docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-energy-shields.md#drawing-the-meter).
- On the TOMAHAWK and the PANTHER, whose weapon rows sit at the top of the screen, the top weapon rows can be left half drawn after the cockpit shakes from a hit: a charge bar keeps stray stripes of an old charge level along its top, specks of a wrong color can appear around its power marker, and a weapon's name or ready light can keep the top half of its old look. While the cockpit shakes, nothing it draws reaches the top few rows of the screen. Reported in HERCULAN Engine's faithful port and matched in the disassembly; not yet checked in the running game. Reproduced as-is; the "Fix graphical glitches when hit" Tweak, off by default and recommended, draws the top rows where the shake has moved them. See `Es1Cockpit.DrawsFollowShake` and [`docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-weapon-gauges.md`](docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-weapon-gauges.md#while-the-cockpit-shakes).
- On the arming and service bay screens, the RHINO's ECM is drawn on its chest though it is fitted to the top centre mount: the HERC's layout file places it there. A damaged EMP on that mount is drawn off to the left of where the undamaged one sits, since its damaged picture is a different drawing placed at the same point. **Fixed** in HERCULAN Engine as the "Fix RHINO top mount art" Tweak, on by default. See `Es1HercArt.FixRhinoTopMount` and [`docs/es1/formats/herc-diagrams.md`](docs/es1/formats/herc-diagrams.md#the-mount-pictures).
- On the service bay and arming screens, a weapon that has been repaired, after a mission or on REPAIR or SYSTEM REPAIR, is still drawn damaged until the career is loaded again: a repair does not redraw the HERC's weapon pictures. Read from the disassembly and seen in HERCULAN Engine's faithful port. Reproduced as-is; the "Fix weapon art after repair" Tweak, off by default and recommended, draws each weapon by its condition as it stands. See `Es1HercDiagrams.RefreshWeaponArt` and [`docs/es1/formats/herc-diagrams.md`](docs/es1/formats/herc-diagrams.md#the-mount-pictures).
- The service bay's scramble lights can stay lit after resuming or restoring a career whose next mission is no longer a scramble: they follow the last mission the game loaded, and loading a career that stands at the service bay after a mission loads none. Read from the disassembly and seen in HERCULAN Engine's faithful port. Reproduced as-is; the "Fix false scramble alarm" Tweak, off by default and recommended, lights them only for the career's own next mission. See `Es1HangarBay.ScrambleLightsFollowCareer` and [`docs/es1/shell/hangar.md`](docs/es1/shell/hangar.md#giving-the-scripts-their-bays).
- A DETAIL setting of MEDIUM on the shell's PREFERENCES screen reads HIGH the next time the game starts, though the game keeps MEDIUM's settings until DETAIL is pressed again. Reproduced as-is. See [`docs/es1/shell/options.md`](docs/es1/shell/options.md#detail-after-a-restart).

### Functional
- On a fast PC the whole game runs at 1.5 times real time. Each simulation step is counted as at least 25 ms, but the game takes the next step after only 16.7 ms. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, which takes a step every 25 ms. See [`docs/es1/simulation/fixed-point-math.md`](docs/es1/simulation/fixed-point-math.md#the-tick-length--time_beginsimtick-00063f40).
- How far autocannon and EMP rounds fly depends on the frame rate: a round's life is counted in frames. **Fixed** in HERCULAN Engine as the "Full cannon range" Tweak, on by default. See [`docs/es1/simulation/projectiles.md`](docs/es1/simulation/projectiles.md#the-reach).
- How far a missile flies depends on the frame rate: its life is counted in frames. On a computer that keeps 40 frames a second it stops after about 210 m, short of the 360 m the launchers fire at; at 30 frames a second it reaches about 340 m, and at 10 frames a second about 2 km. Reproduced at HERCULAN Engine's steady 40 steps a second. See [`docs/es1/simulation/rockets.md`](docs/es1/simulation/rockets.md#the-reach).
- Radar dishes spin, and gun towers turn their turrets, by a fixed step every frame, so both speeds depend on the frame rate. Reproduced at HERCULAN Engine's steady 40 steps a second. See [`docs/es1/simulation/structures.md`](docs/es1/simulation/structures.md#the-plain-tick--base_thinktick-00002df8) and [`docs/es1/simulation/structures.md`](docs/es1/simulation/structures.md#the-turret--base_aimturret-00003058).
- Flyers move and turn the same amount every frame, so the faster the game runs, the faster they fly and turn. **Fixed** in HERCULAN Engine as the "Earthsiege 2 flyer speed" Tweak, on by default, which flies and turns them as Earthsiege 2's flyers do at its frame rate, at any frame rate. See `Es1FlyerFlight.Es2Speed` and [`docs/es1/simulation/flyers.md`](docs/es1/simulation/flyers.md#the-move--flyer_movementtick-0001b084).
- Squadmates and other allied HERCs keep firing at a disabled enemy HERC until it is destroyed when it belongs to the enemy group their orders name, so they shoot HERCs that look dead. Reproduced as-is; the "Squadmates stop shooting disabled HERCs" Tweak, off by default and recommended, lets them move on once it is disabled, as Earthsiege 2's do. See `Es1Targets.DropDisabledOrderTarget` and [`docs/es1/simulation/mech-ai.md`](docs/es1/simulation/mech-ai.md#targets-and-orders).
- Once one squadmate's radio reply fails to play, as it can in a busy fight, no squadmate's reply is heard or shown again for the rest of the mission, though they still carry out orders. Seen in HERCULAN Engine, which reproduces the code; not yet checked in the running game. **Fixed** in HERCULAN Engine as the "Fix unresponsive squadmates" Tweak, on by default. See `Es1MessagePort.ClearDueOnRemoval` and [`docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-messages.md`](docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-messages.md#the-lifecycle).
- When the lead aircraft of a pair is shot down, its wingman stops attacking and flies tight circles where the leader went down, until its target is destroyed: it keeps trying to hold station on the wreck. Reproduced as-is; the "Wingman takes over a flight" Tweak, off by default and recommended, gives the lead to the aircraft still flying. Seen in C3_03 in HERCULAN Engine's faithful port. See `Es1FlyerAi.LiveLeader` and [`docs/es1/simulation/flyers.md`](docs/es1/simulation/flyers.md#the-thinks).
- A recorded demo plays back as fast as the computer can draw it, rather than in real time. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, which plays a demo at the speed it was recorded. See [`docs/es1/simulation/input.md`](docs/es1/simulation/input.md#recording-and-playback).
- On the SYSTEM REPAIR screen, pressing + and then PROCEED repairs the part one level further than was paid for. Reproduced as-is; keeping only the paid levels is an optional Tweak in HERCULAN Engine. See [`docs/es1/shell/repair-and-build.md`](docs/es1/shell/repair-and-build.md#cancel-and-proceed).
- A change made on the in-mission preferences panel (F12) is lost at the next mission if the CONTROLS panel is opened and closed before leaving it. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, which always saves the panel's settings. See [`docs/es1/simulation/preferences.md`](docs/es1/simulation/preferences.md#saving).
- In a single mission, answering PROCEED to the warning that a squadmate's HERC is unarmed should crash the game: the loading screen reads the mission map that PROCEED has just deleted. No single mission is yet known to give a squadmate an unarmed HERC. See [`docs/es1/shell/war-map.md`](docs/es1/shell/war-map.md#the-loading-screen-after-the-unarmed-alert).
- New careers can save over other careers. A career created after deleting one is given the last career's save files, so both careers share them; a second career created before a mission is started replaces the first one in the career list and takes its files; and `names.log` lists a new career only once the game leaves the menus, for a mission or to quit. Reported by a player and matched in the disassembly. Reproduced as-is; the "Fix career save bugs" Tweak, off by default and recommended, gives each new career files of its own and saves the list at once. See `Es1ShellData.FixCareerSaves` and [`docs/es1/formats/career-log-and-player-mec.md`](docs/es1/formats/career-log-and-player-mec.md#adding-a-career).
- A finished career can be played past its end. After the last mission, RESUME and PROCEED show CAREER OVER, but after STATS or SAVE/RESTORE on the career menu they open the service bay instead, and NEXT MISSION > there moves the career on to a campaign that does not exist; what the game then loads is undefined. Read from the disassembly. Reproduced in HERCULAN Engine up to that load, which there starts the last mission again after an empty new-campaign message and talking head, as a player reported. The "Fix replaying a finished career" Tweak, off by default and recommended, shows CAREER OVER on NEXT MISSION > instead. See `Es1ServiceBayMenu.CareerOverStops` and [`docs/es1/shell/between-missions.md`](docs/es1/shell/between-missions.md#moving-on).
- The BUILD screen opens on the HERC it last showed, even one picked or under construction in another career, so with a free bay and enough salvage a HERC can be built before the career unlocks it. The arrows step only through the career's unlocked HERCs, so pressing one loses it. Reported by a player and matched in the disassembly. Reproduced as-is; the "Fix build exploit" Tweak, off by default and recommended, opens BUILD on an unlocked HERC. See `Es1FabricationMenu.ShownChassisFollowsCareer` and [`docs/es1/shell/repair-and-build.md`](docs/es1/shell/repair-and-build.md#build--screen-2000).
- The pilot name box on the REGISTRATION screen can hang the game. Typing after moving the cursor back lengthens the name past what the box shows, and a click near the box's right edge then never finishes. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, where such a click moves the text along and stops. See `Es1MTEditBox.Invalidate` and [`docs/es1/shell/career.md`](docs/es1/shell/career.md#past-the-end).
- On the war map, `.` or `,` hangs the game when no map icon is in view and none has been picked yet. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine. See [`docs/es1/shell/map.md`](docs/es1/shell/map.md#picking-a-gadget).
- The launcher crashes at startup when the `PREFER_ALT` environment variable is set without `ALT_DRIVE`. HERCULAN Engine reports the setting and stops. See [`docs/es1/shell/launcher.md`](docs/es1/shell/launcher.md#starting--launcher_main-000005cc).
- The memory manager's check for a write past the end of a block never looks past the block, as in Earthsiege 2. No bearing on HERCULAN Engine, which has its own memory management. See [`docs/es1/simulation/memory.md`](docs/es1/simulation/memory.md).

### Input
- Without a calibration file (`data\joy1.cal`), the joystick works on only one side of each axis, so the HERC turns one way only. Not applicable to HERCULAN Engine, which reads no calibration (below). See [`docs/es1/simulation/joystick.md`](docs/es1/simulation/joystick.md#the-calibration-file).
- Choosing `NONE` for the joystick on the CONTROLS panel switches the device it has just freed back on instead of off; what the stick then does is not established. The same happens to a second stick no joystick model needs. **Not reproduced** in HERCULAN Engine, where such a stick gets no device: `NONE` leaves no stick working, and an unneeded second stick is not read. See `Es1JoystickDevices.Rebuild` and [`docs/es1/simulation/preferences.md`](docs/es1/simulation/preferences.md#rebuilding-the-joystick-devices--controlspanel_applydevices-00048e98).
- The `Z` key, which the manual does not mention, is meant to set every energy weapon's power level to one fixed setting, but it changes nothing, as in Earthsiege 2. Read from the disassembly, not yet checked in retail. HERCULAN Engine has no `Z` key. See [`docs/retail/simulation/weapon-firing.md`](docs/retail/simulation/weapon-firing.md#power-level--weaponmount_adjustpowerlevel-0040f48c).

## HERCULAN Engine

_Note to Claude: This section is for listing features which have been implemented but behave differently than retail or otherwise incorrectly. Features that haven't been tackled yet go in [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md). Bugs in retail belong in the previous section, with HERCULAN Engine's handling of them. Detailed technical descriptions belong in documentation, not here. Give a short plain-English summary._

- **(By design)** The depth test decides which of two overlapping zone shapes, HERCs, rounds or effects shows. Retail paints its object list farthest first with no depth test, so there the object it paints later shows even where it lies farther away. The shadows keep retail's order, painted before every object. See `Es1WorldDraw` and [`docs/es1/rendering/polygon-shading.md`](docs/es1/rendering/polygon-shading.md#the-frames-draw).
  - A click on the gunsight follows it too: it picks the object the depth test shows at the pointer, where retail's picks the one it paints there last ([plan decision 16](docs/herculan/plan-es1-first-mission.md#decisions)).
- **(By design)** The MFD's NAV MAP draws the zone's shapes at the window's resolution and with the depth test, so they are sharper than retail's, which draws them in the screen's own pixels, each over the last in the zone file's order, so there a shape listed later shows even where it lies lower. The map's background and cross keep retail's colours. The missile camera's view from a flown missile is drawn the same way. See `Es1VideoMemory.DrawWorldView`, [plan decision 17](docs/herculan/plan-es1-first-mission.md#decisions), [`docs/es1/simulation/mfd-nav-map.md`](docs/es1/simulation/mfd-nav-map.md#the-paint) and [`docs/es1/simulation/mfd-missile-camera.md`](docs/es1/simulation/mfd-missile-camera.md#painting).
  - Its shapes are lit by the sun alone. Retail picks no lights for the map, so its shapes also take the explosion lights left over from the last object drawn in the 3D view, as they were then. Sun-only is most likely what retail meant. The command screen's map is lit the same way, which shows no difference, since it draws every lit face in one colour. See `Es1MissionHost.DrawRectView` and [`docs/es1/rendering/polygon-shading.md`](docs/es1/rendering/polygon-shading.md#effect-lights).
- The world shows a palette change from the next time it is drawn. Retail's whole screen is palette indices, so a change recolors the world with the cockpit. Here the main view takes each step of the death flash's fade, and the sky's shading as the view pitches, one frame late, and a cockpit display's world, the NAV MAP's, the missile camera's or the Heads-Down Display map's, when it is next painted. The hit flash shows no difference, since those displays draw in colors the flash palette shares. See `Es1WorldPalette` and [plan decision 18](docs/herculan/plan-es1-first-mission.md#decisions).
- **(By design)** Each pass of the lift's ride up to the surface takes 25 ms and the next retrace, so the ride lasts about 3.3 s. Retail's pass took as long as the machine took to draw it, since its wait for the monitor ends at once inside a retrace; on a machine that draws faster than a retrace the ride lasts about 1.6 s. See `Es1LiftStart.PassDrawTime` and [plan decision 24](docs/herculan/plan-es1-first-mission.md#decisions).
- **(By design)** A joystick needs no calibration: a modern controller's travel arrives as a calibrated stick's, −128 to +128 on each of the game port's four axes, with its roles wired onto the port's lines (stick X and Y, rudder, throttle, and the hat where the chosen stick model sent one). Retail times the port's axis lines in port reads and maps them through a calibration the player records on its calibration panel and keeps in `data\joy1.cal`. The panel and the files are not read, so the CONTROLS panel's CALIBRATE only rebuilds the devices. See `Es1ControllerPort`, [plan decision 9](docs/herculan/plan-es1-first-mission.md#decisions) and [`docs/es1/simulation/joystick.md`](docs/es1/simulation/joystick.md#normalising-an-axis).
- **(By design)** The shell's typed characters, the letters of a new career's name among them, are the ones the host's keyboard layout types. Retail leaves the keys its menus do not claim to the PC BIOS, whose US table makes the characters, so there `Ctrl` or `Alt` with a letter types a control or extended code, where here it mostly types nothing. See `Es1BiosKeyBuffer` and [plan decision 22](docs/herculan/plan-es1-first-mission.md#decisions).
- **(By design)** `Alt+Enter` switches between full screen and a window, in the shell, the movies and a mission, and the game starts the way it was last left. The game never sees that `Enter`, so a dialog or panel that `Enter` closes stays up. Retail, a DOS program, has no such key and takes it as `Enter` with `Alt` held. See `Es1FullScreenKey` and `Es1FullScreen`.
- **(By design)** The disc the Settings menu names, a folder or a disc image (`.iso`, `.bin` or `.cue`), stands in for the CD drive when the environment sets no `ALT_DRIVE`: the launcher plays the intro and end flics from it, and the shell and the missions read the archives a smaller install left on it. Retail finds the CD through the installer's `ES.BAT`, which names the CD's drive letter in a full install and changes to that drive in a minimum or medium one; HERCULAN reads which of the two `ES.BAT` sets up and keeps the disc itself. See `Es1Disc`, `Es1EsBat` and [`docs/es1/shell/launcher.md`](docs/es1/shell/launcher.md#the-installers-esbat).
  - A minimum or medium install started with no disc kept asks for the disc before it runs. Retail's `ES.BAT` stops at the empty CD drive. See `Es1DiscPrompt` and [plan decision 46](docs/herculan/plan-es1-first-mission.md#decisions).
- **(By design)** The install window writes `C:` for the install and `D:` for the CD into `ES.BAT`, `data\addr.bat` and `RESOURCE.CFG`, where retail's installer writes the drives it was run with, so the install runs in DOS with the install mounted as `C:` and the CD as `D:`. HERCULAN plays from the disc whatever drive it is on. See `Es1Installer`.
- **(By design)** In a window wider than 4:3 a side glance slides the view only until the side panel's outer edge meets the window's edge, and jumps the rest of the way at once. Retail slides the whole panel, 10 columns per screen refresh, about half a second. The "High resolution widescreen" tweak. See `Es1ViewManager.GlanceReach` and [plan decision 30](docs/herculan/plan-es1-first-mission.md#decisions).
