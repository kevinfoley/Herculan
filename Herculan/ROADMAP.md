# Roadmap — work not yet done

Everything the HERCULAN Engine does not implement yet, in one place. This is the counterpart of [`KNOWN_ISSUES.md`](KNOWN_ISSUES.md), which records things that *are* implemented but behave differently from retail. If a feature is missing entirely it belongs here; if it is present and wrong it belongs there.

Each entry names the doc that owns the subject. **That doc is authoritative** for how much is reverse-engineered and how much is still unknown — this file only tracks that the porting work is outstanding.

## Reverse-engineered, not ported

The mechanism is understood; what is left is engine work.

## Reverse-engineering still open

The engine cannot be faithful here until the original is understood.

- **How many times DBSIM has advanced its generator before any given roll.** The algorithm, the 56-entry seed table and both cursor starts are ported, so the two generators produce identical streams from the same starting point — but a roll's result depends on its position in that stream, and this engine does not yet make the same draws in the same order. Replay parity needs the call history matched, which is really a question about tick order, not about the generator. → [`docs/retail/simulation/random-generator.md`](docs/retail/simulation/random-generator.md)

## The shell front end
The front end draws the frame every tab screen shares — the tiled backdrop, the square button and the eight captioned tabs, hit-tested, latching on the six tabs that latch, gated by campaign mode and switching palette per tab — plus the startup sequence and the main menu it brings up, whose `INSTANT ACTION` flies the next demo mission, `START NEW GAME` starts a campaign from its registration screen, `CONTINUE GAME` loads the current game and `VIEW DEMO` plays a demo tape, its practice screen, whose `Begin Mission` flies the lit training mission, and the save, weapons, repair, build, armory and crew screens behind tabs 0 to 6, weapon fitting included, with the squad panel down the left of four of them, and the mission tab's campaign map and its briefing with its map, whose `Rock & Roll >` launches the mission, and the way back from it — the debrief's accounting and its report, then the next mission's load or the offer to replay the one that ended the campaign — all with the shell's music and click sounds, and its movies: the intro, the campaign map's with its location picture, the briefing's and `CREDITS`. The widget paints are ported onto an indexed software canvas, so a further screen is layout, text and hit-testing rather than new drawing code. What is missing:
- **The startup's `Performance Note` box.** Retail shows it once; the shell does not. → [`docs/retail/shell/main-menu.md`](docs/retail/shell/main-menu.md#the-main-menu)

## Metaltech: Earthsiege
Metaltech: Earthsiege loads a single mission into its zone viewer, where the player's HERC walks, runs and turns its turret under the keyboard or a joystick on ES1's tick, the cockpit's views, glances, outside views and pointer work, the gunsight draws and enemies the sensor model reports can be targeted, the throttle gauge follows the throttle and sets it when dragged, the computer's ticker announces each waypoint reached, the player's weapons fire, refire and recharge, missiles home and can be flown from the missile camera, shots and blasts damage HERCs through their shields down to their components until they are disabled or destroyed, with explosions, debris and smoke, mission actions release the groups they hold, the AI's HERCs walk their routes and formations, fight, fire, answer fire and take the squad's orders, the mission's status is polled and put up, and its end writes the results, the campaign flags and the exit code for the shell, and the pause and quit prompts, the objectives and the preferences and controls panels come up; the order the rest arrives in is [`docs/herculan/plan-es1-first-mission.md`](docs/herculan/plan-es1-first-mission.md). What is missing:
- **Metaltech: Earthsiege's cockpit instruments.** The canopy art, the throttle gauge, the gunsight, the clock with its HERC's `.TTM` script, the weapon rows, the console, the energy meter, the shield display, the MFD's six screens and the Heads-Down Display with the squadmates' comm boxes and its command screen are drawn; the display's damage screen draws nothing and takes no press or key. → [`docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-panel.md`](docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-panel.md), [`docs/es1/simulation/hdd-damage-screen.md`](docs/es1/simulation/hdd-damage-screen.md)
- **Metaltech: Earthsiege's hit flash.** A hit shakes the cockpit view without the palette's flicker, since the theater's impact palette is not loaded, and the effects' billboards, the sky's bands, the skyline and the clouds keep the colors they were built in, so the death flash does not reach them either. → [`docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-panel.md`](docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-panel.md#the-hit-shake)
- **Metaltech: Earthsiege's sound.** The footfalls, the fall, the refused move's thump, the turret's servo, the engine hum, the adaptive music, the pilots' voices, the comm boxes' static, the display buttons' click, the weapons, the explosions and the lock tone play nothing, though the latches that start them run. → [`docs/es1/simulation/main-loop.md`](docs/es1/simulation/main-loop.md#the-frames-sounds--sim_framesoundtick-00058574)
- **Metaltech: Earthsiege's input tapes.** `-p`, `-r` and `-D` are not taken, so no mission is recorded or played back and the demo mode never runs. → [`docs/es1/simulation/input.md`](docs/es1/simulation/input.md#recording-and-playback)
- **Metaltech: Earthsiege's joystick calibration.** The calibration panel and the `data\joy1.cal` and `joy2.cal` files it saves are not read, a modern controller's travel standing in for a calibrated stick's. → [`docs/es1/simulation/joystick.md`](docs/es1/simulation/joystick.md#calibrating)
- **A modern controller's hat in Metaltech: Earthsiege.** The hat drives no port line until a retail check settles which of the device record's hat bytes is which direction. → [`docs/es1/simulation/joystick.md`](docs/es1/simulation/joystick.md#hats-and-throttles)
- **Metaltech: Earthsiege's lift.** A mission whose HERC starts within 40,000 units (240 m) of a friendly structure opens without retail's lift, whose two calls are unread. → [`docs/es1/simulation/main-loop.md`](docs/es1/simulation/main-loop.md#the-mission-start--sim_startmission-0005a9dc)
- **Metaltech: Earthsiege's start-of-run files.** The simulator's start writes no `data\mem.got` and no 0 to `data\exit.cfg`, so a run the window's close ends leaves `exit.cfg` as the last mission's end wrote it. → [`docs/es1/simulation/main-loop.md`](docs/es1/simulation/main-loop.md#starting--sim_main-00059350)
- **Metaltech: Earthsiege's cockpit colors past scheme 7.** A scheme above 7, whose word retail reads from past the end of its table, keeps the second range's color at `0x40`. → [`docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-views.md`](docs/es1/simulation/cockpit-views.md#the-color-scheme)
- **Metaltech: Earthsiege's after-mission files and career load.** A single mission's load writes no `car\sm_play.mec` or `car\sm_play.gam` for the shell to read back after the mission, and a career's load waits for the shell. → [`docs/es1/formats/msn-mission-file.md`](docs/es1/formats/msn-mission-file.md#the-squad)

## Debugging features
- **A launch option that disables the AI.** Every unit but the player's stays stationary, though it can still be damaged and destroyed. → [`docs/herculan/herculan-command-line.md`](docs/herculan/herculan-command-line.md#developer)
- **Editing the current `script.dat` in the Editor**, chiefly moving Cybrid and player spawn points, to set up test scenarios quickly, and showing the rest of a mission's wiring there (groups, orders, objectives, timers). The scope is in [`docs/herculan/handoff-editor-mission-editing.md`](docs/herculan/handoff-editor-mission-editing.md). → [`docs/retail/formats/script-dat.md`](docs/retail/formats/script-dat.md)
- (Deferred) **Exact replay of an engine recording.** `--record` writes tapes `--play` replays, but the tick length, pointer rounding, held keys and a handful of inputs the tape does not carry let a replay drift from its recording; each is a choice of how this engine records, fixable without further RE. A check that catches the drift would be this engine's own design: retail's `-d` checkpoint routine has no known caller, so what it snapshots, and when, is not known. → [`docs/herculan/input-tapes.md`](docs/herculan/input-tapes.md#what-a-replay-of-an-engine-recording-does-not-reproduce), [`docs/retail/formats/tap-input-tape.md`](docs/retail/formats/tap-input-tape.md#the-checkpoint-file)

## HERCULAN's own interface
- (Deferred) **Interface languages in other scripts.** Every ImGui window builds its font atlas from Open Sans with ImGui's default glyph ranges, Latin and Latin-1 only (each `new ImGuiFontConfig(path, 16)` in `Herculan.Engine.Host` and the editor), so a `.lang` file in Cyrillic or another script would draw its text as missing glyphs. Worth doing once such a file exists: pass the ranges it needs, and check the font carries them. → [`LocalizationTable.cs`](src/Herculan.Engine.Host/Localization/LocalizationTable.cs)

## Platform
- **Following a newly connected audio output.** Sound moves to a new default output only when the old one disconnects, so headphones plugged in while the speakers play are not followed. The handler for `ALC_SOFT_system_events`' default-changed event is in place, but that extension arrived in OpenAL Soft 1.24 and the bundled runtime is 1.23.1: Silk.NET's 2.23.0 release notes name 1.24.3, yet its `Silk.NET.OpenAL.Soft.Native` package has not been published past 1.23.1. Moving to that package once it is enables the handler, which then needs a plug-in check. → [`OpenAlDeviceWatch.cs`](src/Herculan.Engine/Audio/OpenAlDeviceWatch.cs)

## HercWorks editors
- **Edit the configuration files and zone headers in HercWorks UI.** Core reads and writes `data\keyjoy.cfg` (`KeyjoyTransformer`), `data\prefs.cfg` (`PrefsTransformer`), `data\drive.cfg` (`DriveTransformer`) and `dat\zoneNNNN.dat` (`ZoneDatTransformer`), but the UI has no form for any of them. Two things to settle first: `keyjoy.cfg`'s writer drops the comments a retail file ships with, and `prefs.cfg` should be written back as the simulator does — a read-modify-write of the bytes the form changed, never a fresh 54-byte dump. → [`docs/retail/formats/keyjoy-cfg.md`](docs/retail/formats/keyjoy-cfg.md), [`docs/retail/simulation/preferences.md`](docs/retail/simulation/preferences.md), [`docs/retail/formats/zone-terrain.md`](docs/retail/formats/zone-terrain.md)
